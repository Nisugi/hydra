Module goals 

Source
Expand description

A character’s skill goals as the play.net skill manager holds them, and the arithmetic of changing them.

The arithmetic is the website’s own (its upskill and downskill): a rank costs its skill’s physical and mental points, doubled past the character’s adjusted level (level + 2, or + 1 at the cap of 100) and quadrupled past twice that; a pool short of points takes them from the other at two for one, and lowering a skill hands converted points back first, two for each one returned. The website checks everything again when goals are applied: this is so the totals the trainer shows are the ones the website would.
