The route, shown and not walked (.route2), and where a target is.

Showing the way rather than walking it is how a wrong or missing exit in the map is found: the table says, room by room, what would be taken and what was not.

§One pricing for every route

The way shown, the way walked and “the nearest bank” are all priced by pricing: an exit is open only when this character can take it – the map’s own questions about it, a soulstone, a profession, a setting – and Hydra walks it (crate::coverage::walked). Finding the nearest anything, or any route at all, tries only paths this character can actually take. What Hydra does not walk yet is shut, and said so under the room it leaves.

§What the table shows

A scripted exit is shown as its steps, and a scripted cost is counted: a crossing is a list of steps and a cost is a question asked of the walker, so both are shown and the total is the real one. And it says what it did not take: at each room on the way, every exit shut to this walker, with the reason – a fact the game has not told yet, a fact that says no, or no cost in the map.

Pure: a map and a walker in, rows out. table lays them out as text for a notice; the minimap takes the rooms.
