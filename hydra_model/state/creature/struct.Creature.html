Expand description

What the bestiary knows about one creature.

Every field but Self::id and Self::name may be absent: the templates are a community catalogue, filled in as people measure things.

Fields§

§id: String

The template’s file stem: albino_tomb_spider.

§name: String

The display name: albino tomb spider.

§noun: Option<String>

The wire noun a command targets it by: spider.

§level: Option<i32>

Creature level, where known.

§max_hp: Option<Stat>

Typical maximum hit points.

§family: Option<String>

Arachnid, Bear, Gigas.

§kind: Option<String>

Quadruped, Biped, Ooze.

§size: Option<String>

small, medium, large, huge.

§height: Option<i32>

Height in feet, where known.

§speed: Option<Stat>

Speed, as the bestiary records it.

§undead: Option<bool>

Is it undead?

§blood: Option<bool>

Corporeal, flesh and blood.

§bones: Option<bool>

Has a skeletal structure.

§limbs: Option<bool>

Has limbs that Limb Disruption (708) can target.

§witherable: Option<bool>

Has a body that Wither (1115) can attack.

§sympathy: Option<bool>

Can be affected by Sympathy (1120).

§muggable: Option<bool>

Carries coin worth mugging.

§sleepable: Option<bool>

Can be put to sleep. Unknown for 342 of 627.

§bcs: Option<bool>

The template’s bcs flag, three-valued; the template does not say what it stands for.

§boss: bool

Is a boss encounter.

§boss_type: Option<String>

pack, miniboss, boss, where classified.

§other_class: Vec<String>

Extra classifications: Living, Construct, Spirit.

§asg: Option<String>

Armor sub-group as the bestiary writes it: 12N, 1, 8N.

A String rather than a number because the N suffix is part of the value and 167 templates record none at all.

§melee_ds: Option<Stat>

Defensive strength against melee.

§ranged_ds: Option<Stat>

Defensive strength against ranged.

§bolt_ds: Option<Stat>

Defensive strength against bolts.

§udf: Option<Stat>

Unarmed defense factor.

§target_defense: BTreeMap<String, Stat>

Target defense by spell circle, keyed as the bestiary keys it: the professions’ bar_td .. wiz_td, and the minor and major circles mje_td, mne_td, mjs_td, mns_td, mnm_td.

A map rather than twelve named fields: a caster wants their own circle’s number, and a consumer asking for one it does not know about should get None rather than fail to compile.

§defensive_spells: Vec<String>

Spells it has up in its own defense.

§defensive_abilities: Vec<Entry>

Defensive abilities, with the bestiary’s note on each.

§immunities: Vec<String>

What it cannot be harmed by, or cannot be affected by.

§special_defenses: Vec<String>

Defenses that fit nowhere else.

§treasure: Treasure

What it drops.

§equipment: Vec<String>

What it is seen carrying, from its LOOK gear line.

§areas: Vec<Area>

Where it is found.

§attacks: Vec<Attack>

Every attack, of every category.

§special_notes: Vec<String>

Notes on how it fights that fit nowhere else.

§special_other: Option<String>

Anything else the bestiary says that has no field.

§abilities: Vec<Ability>

Its declared abilities.

§messages: BTreeMap<MessageKind, Vec<Message>>

Every line it prints, by kind.

§info: BTreeMap<String, Vec<String>>

Tips written about it: general, miscellany, or a profession.

§url: Option<String>

Its wiki page.

§picture: Option<String>

A picture of it, where the bestiary links one.

§schema_version: Option<u32>

The template schema this creature was written against.
