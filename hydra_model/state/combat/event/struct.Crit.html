Expand description

A critical, as recorded on the hit that produced it.

The crit table entry’s facts, without its pattern. The coup de grace has no table row – its success line IS the killing blow – and is recorded as a synthetic fatal at the struck location.

Fields§

§location: Location

Where it landed.

§damage_type: Option<DamageType>

The table’s damage type, or None for the coup’s synthetic crit.

§rank: Option<u8>

Severity 0..=9 (None for the coup).

§wound_rank: Option<u8>

The wound left, 0..=3 (None for the coup).

§fatal: bool

The target died of this.

§amputated: bool

A limb came off.

§stunned: u16

Rounds of stun; see CritEntry::stun_is_known.

§roundtime: u8

Extra roundtime, seconds.

§position: Option<Position>

Knocked to a floor position.

§silenced: bool

Silenced.

§slowed: bool

Slowed.

§dazed: bool

Dazed.

§sleeping: bool

Put to sleep.

§crippled: bool

Crippled.

§limb_favored: bool

Favouring a limb.

§secondary_wound: Option<SecondaryWound>

A second location wounded by the same crit.

§line: usize

Where the crit text sat in the chunk: crit statuses are applied after the whole chunk is parsed, so this is the only record of where the crit stood relative to the messages around it (position_recovered?).
