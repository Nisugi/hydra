hydra_model::state::ship

Module reports 

Source
Expand description

The reports the game prints of ships and the trade: SHIP INFO, bare and of one hull, OSA INFO, OSA TASK, OSA ROLE, OSA FINES, REPUTATION KRAKEN, a dock handler’s weather, and the captain’s map.

Each is read from the chunk its prompt closed, and only from a chunk that holds the report’s own opening line. A report replaces what the last one of its kind said.
