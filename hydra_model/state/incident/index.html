The lines a hunt reacts to that are not combat: a weapon knocked away, a curse or infection taken, a trap sprung, an ambusher, rooted, a bless gone, an arrow stuck, a charge counter, a mark on a creature, the weapon reaction offered.

21 events in seven families, gathered into one table. The state machine never reads them; the hunt does.

§Plain text and the line’s objects, not the markup

Each pattern matches the line’s plain text and the id and noun come from the line’s objects (text.rs): an item is the first unbolded object, a creature the first bolded one (bold is the wire’s mark for a creature). The weapon reaction’s command is the <d cmd=> link’s own. So nothing is re-parsed.

The weapon is the object right after your, or the line is not one. The game’s weapon lines carry the weapon’s link in that place, which is the half of them a player’s words cannot supply. The plain text alone matches Bob says, "Your sword tears free from your hands and floats away.", and the first unbolded object then is Bob. Spoken lines do not reach here at all (ChunkLine::is_spoken); the anchor is kept as well.

§Queued, and drained by whoever acts

A chunk’s incidents are queued at its prompt (Incidents::take), capped so a session with no hunt running holds a bounded few; the hunt’s driver drains its own fold’s queue each turn.
