Module highlights 

Source
Expand description

The highlights on the page: each listed with what the page edits of it, and the page’s changes made as the window’s editor makes them, at once to every story and to the file with the next batch of saves.

The page edits all of a highlight: its words, looks, hiding, redirect, sound, banner and notification, and its trigger parts – what it sends, the condition it acts under, its timer, flag and status, and the rest. A send written on the page is the one approved, as the player wrote it.
