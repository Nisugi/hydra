hydra_travel::walk

Module drive 

Source
Expand description

The thin async layer round Trip: what has the socket and the clock. It joins the session (SessionHandle::join), keeps its own GameState by folding the frames that follow, says where the character is, sends what the trip says to send, does its Deeds (deeds), and stops when told.

§When it stops

  • Stopped: .stop, .go2 stop, or another .go2.
  • Handed to Lich: one of the player’s Lich scripts sent a move while Hydra walked. Hydra hands over control rather than fight it: there is no telling whether that script will go on moving the character, and two walkers pulling one character apart get neither where they mean to go.
  • Lost: the connection dropped or the session ended. A walk does not outlive the connection it set out on, and sends nothing after.
  • Halted: a rule of the walk stopped it, such as stop_for_dead beside someone dead.

A walk that cannot tell where it is for LOST_WAIT ends, rather than wait for ever.

§Nothing is sent into roundtime

A search is followed by roundtime, and so are climbs and many other steps: a command sent into it is only told to wait. So after any single command – a search, a climb, a step of a crossing – nothing more is sent until the game’s prompt has closed its answer (ANSWER_WAIT at most), which is when its roundtime is known; and then nothing until the roundtime is over. Plain moves sent ahead in a batch cause none, and a batch begins only out of roundtime.

§What a stop sends

A stop is not a reason to send more, with one exception: what a crossing put away is asked back, one command each and nothing else, since where the walker stands it may not even be able to hold a shield; and a key a crossing took out goes back first, so the walk finishes up and leaves the character safe. Nothing is waited for. What was not seen back is in Walked, for the player to be told. A stance or a language is not put back on a stop. A walk that lost its connection sends nothing at all.
