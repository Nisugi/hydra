Module walk 

Source
Expand description

The walk, as a pure state machine: Trip::tick is told where the walker is and what time it is, and answers with what to send. It owns no socket and reads no clock, so every rule below is tested by table; the driver that feeds it the game and sends what it asks is drive, thin by design.

It crosses plain exits, passes through an urchin hub, every list of steps the map gives an exit, and every named routine: the searches, puzzles and errands no list of steps can say. A routine is a pure solver (routines); the driver runs it, and its moves come back through the trip as steps beside the plan (Trip::aside).

§Arrival is where the walker is, not what the game said

A move has arrived when the located room is the one expected. Landing anywhere else is a fact to plan from: the trip plans again from wherever it now is.

§When a move does not simply work

The driver hands every line hydra_model::movement can name to Trip::heard. Nothing is done with it there: it waits for the next tick, so that a room change is always looked at first. A failure line that raced an arrival belongs to a move that worked, and acting on one sends the walker somewhere it never meant to go. The remedies and their budgets are in recovery; the rules round them:

  • what is sent again is what was sent, never the map’s text, since a remedy may have rewritten it;
  • after an exit is given up there is an orphan window in which lines are ignored, because they are about the move just abandoned;
  • silence is not failure: a move that gets no answer is sent again a couple of times, and the exit is banned only if the trip has never left its first room. Lag further along must never cost the map an exit.

§Crossings that are lists of steps

run runs them, and a plain exit is the one-step list [move], so there is one way across. What the trip cannot spell as a command it hands to the driver as a Deed. Whatever a crossing changed is owed back before the trip says it is over – hands, stance, language, a key – on arrival, and on failure (Trip::owed).

Modules§

drive
The thin async layer round Trip: what has the socket and the clock. It joins the session (SessionHandle::join), keeps its own GameState by folding the frames that follow, says where the character is, sends what the trip says to send, does its Deeds (deeds), and stops when told.
instability
The way into the Elemental Confluence: the map has no exit into the plane, only the instabilities that open on it, which wander. Pure: the game’s answers and the map in, rooms and facts out; the searching is the driver’s (drive::confluence).
