hydra_travel::walk

Module preflight 

Source
Expand description

What must be known before a trip is priced: a cost never acts. A cost only reads a fact, and never sends a command while the pathfinder runs, so whatever a fact needs asked is asked once, before the first plan (super::drive’s pre-flight). Pure: the map and the game’s answers in, facts out.

Functions§

gigas_least
The gigas fragments the Travel page keeps back: a walk spends them only with at least this many carried. Read from settings’s gigas_min_number, four until set. One reading, so .go2 spends and .route2 says by the same count.
gigas_way
The way by gigas fragments a walk from here to goal along path (from here) would take instead, when path takes the long way: into the Hinterwilds by the teleporter – Ta’Illistim’s when path passes Seethe Naedal, the Abbey’s otherwise – or out by Sparkfinger’s workroom. Arriving or leaving is the goal’s alone: a goal in the Hinterwilds is arriving, any other leaving, wherever the walk sets out from. A walk from Mist Harbor’s bank back through Cold River and out by the caravan starts outside the Hinterwilds, and still leaves by the workroom. A walk with both ends inside takes no fragments. Whether the fragments are there to spend is the caller’s to ask.
out_of_a_shop
The way out of a player’s shop, which no map holds, for a walker standing in room, a room the map does not know: out – after a step further in by its first exit, when the room has no out. None when room is no player’s shop by its title or its number.
silver_for
The silver a walk asks for: each room’s silver-cost:<to>:<silver> tag for the room that follows it. path begins where the walker stands. A cost that does not parse costs nothing here rather than a guess (MEASURED: all 88 of the map’s parse).
withdraw_command
What to say to a banker for this much: Pinefar’s has no withdraw, and hands out no less than twenty. Never for nothing: the least asked is one.
