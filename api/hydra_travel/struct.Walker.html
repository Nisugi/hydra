Expand description

What pricing and guards may ask about the walker. None and a missing key both mean the model has not been told.

Fields§

§settings: HashMap<String, String>

The travel profile: ice_mode → auto. A setting the profile does not carry is unknown; its default belongs to the profile, not to the map.

§memories: HashMap<String, String>

What earlier crossings wrote down (Action::Remember): which town an event ground or Mist Harbor was entered from, which side of the Red Forest. Named as upstream names them, less the mapdb_ prefix (DECIDED, author, 2026-09-20: use Lich’s list – these are not added often). Kept per character and across logins: a character enters Duskruin on Friday and leaves on Sunday. A name never written is unknown, which prices the way back impassable – correctly.

§tables: HashMap<String, HashMap<u32, f64>>

Prices the planner works out, by table and then by room (Cost::Table). instability: seconds from the instability the walker entered the Confluence by to each town – leaving the plane puts it back there, so that walk is what the way out really costs. Filled when the walker enters, by one search from that room. A table or a room that is absent is unknown, and the exit is impassable.

§flags: HashMap<String, bool>

Yes-or-no facts the planner works out, by name: urchin_access (the guides are paid for and have not expired – a comparison against the clock, which this crate never reads), hidden, invisible, mounted; premium_account (the account, which the urchins’ premium hall asks about); hunting (a hunting or wandering behaviour holds the character, which some exits must not be wandered through); platinum (the Platinum instance, where two shops swap doors). A name that is absent is unknown.

§profession: Option<String>

As the game spells it: Bard.

§race: Option<String>

As the game spells it: Forest Gnome. Asked about by a word of it.

§gender: Option<String>

As the game spells it; compared exactly by [Cond::Gender].

§level: Option<u32>

The walker’s experience level.

§citizenship: Option<String>

The town, as the game spells it: Wehnimer's Landing. Some("") is a walker known to be a citizen of nowhere; None is not known.

§society: Option<String>

Order of Voln. Some("") is known to belong to none.

§society_rank: Option<u32>

The walker’s rank within society.

§posture: Option<String>

standing, sitting, kneeling, prone.

§worn: Option<HashSet<String>>

Full names of what the walker wears or carries loose – the top level of its inventory, not what is inside containers. None until listed.

§worn_nouns: Option<HashSet<String>>

The nouns of the same things: key. Known when worn is.

§exits: Option<Vec<String>>

The obvious exits of the room the walker is in, as the game lists them short: ne, nw, up, out.

§room: Option<u32>

The Lich id of the room the walker is in, when the walk knows it.

§sees: Option<Vec<String>>

Full names of the things the room shows: its objects, and the features its description links. None until the room has been read.

§still_here: Option<bool>

Whether the walker is still in the room this crossing began in. Only the walk knows, and only part-way through a crossing; while planning it is None.

§month: Option<u32>

The month of the game’s calendar day, 1-12. A fact passed in, never read from a clock here, so a replay plans the same route.

§encumbrance: Option<u32>

Percent of capacity carried.

§skills: Option<HashMap<String, u32>>

Ranks by skill name, lowercase. None until the skills are known at all, so an untrained skill (absent from a known table) is zero. Spell circles are here too, by their names: major elemental.

§active_spells: Option<HashSet<String>>

Names of spells in effect. None until the game has listed them.

§known_spells: Option<HashSet<String>>

Names of everything the walker can cast: spells, and society powers such as sigils. None until known.

§affordable_spells: Option<HashSet<String>>

Of those, the ones it can pay for now – mana, stamina or spirit, whichever the spell takes. Working that out needs the spell table and the vitals, which is the planner’s business; this crate is told.
