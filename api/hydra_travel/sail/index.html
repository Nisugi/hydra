Sailing the character’s own ship: making it ready and putting to sea.

§Making ready is a list read off the ship, not a script

MakeReady is asked what to do next, shown the model as it stands, and answers with one line to send. It keeps no place in a list: each time it looks at what the game has said of the ship – the flag flown, the gangplank, the sail, the anchor, the deck the character is on – and does the first thing still undone. So it can be started anywhere aboard or on the pier beside the ship, with any of it done already, and a step the game refuses is seen in the state and tried again or passed over. It only counts what it has sent, so nothing is sent for ever.

The order is the one a captain keeps: the white flag, which keeps other ships off and can only be raised from the crow’s nest; the gangplank in; the sail let out, and wind called into it by a character who can; the anchor up; and DEPART, said twice.

Voyage then sails it to the port, and sail is the loop that runs both against a session: send, wait for the game’s prompt and for the roundtime to pass, ask again.

§Or the player sails

With the player at the wheel (Helm::Player) nothing is sent at all: the course is worked out from where the ship is each time it moves, and shown, until the ship lies at the port.
