The swims of Sailor’s Grief: four places with open water between them, and no way from one to another but through it.

The water is one room to the map, many to the game: a grid of [Spicers' Sea, Waters], swum a square at a time. Three of the places lie still on it (Place::lies), so the strokes from one of them to another are known (strokes). The Contempt is a ship, and drifts from anchorage to anchorage: a swim to or from it is found by sense, which says which way the swimmer lies from a place (sensed), a stroke at a time. Every swim ends by sensing, the known strokes too: the place is swum into only when the game says it is a short swim away.

Pure: the places, the strokes and the reading of sense. Sending them is the journey’s.
