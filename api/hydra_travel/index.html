Crate hydra_travel 

Source
Expand description

Travel: walking a character anywhere the map can reach. Every crossing the map holds is to be walked – plain exits, lists of steps, and every named routine – and coverage counts what is not walked yet, down to none.

  • facts: what the map may ask of a character, read from its model (knows: the spells it knows and can pay for).
  • settings: the travel settings, on the character’s Travel page.
  • file: travel.json – memories, the last room, and saved targets.
  • route: where a target is, the way there, and the list of places.
  • command: what the player types: .go2 and .route2.
  • walk: the walk itself, and the driver that sends it to the game.

The map, its conditions and its pathfinder come from the cena-map crate, which the minimap reads too.

The swims of Sailor’s Grief: four places with open water between them, and no way from one to another but through it.
walk
The walk, as a pure state machine: Trip::tick is told where the walker is and what time it is, and answers with what to send. It owns no socket and reads no clock, so every rule below is tested by table; the driver that feeds it the game and sends what it asks is drive, thin by design.
