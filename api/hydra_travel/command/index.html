hydra_travel

Module command 

Source
Expand description

What a player types to travel: .go2 and .route2, after Hydra’s command symbol.

.go2 bank                 walk to the nearest bank
.go2 228   .go2 u7120     ...to a room, by the map's number or the game's
.go2 to Ashryn            ...to the room another character running here is in
.go2 targets              the places there are to go
.go2 list                 the names you have saved
.go2 save den             this room is "den", for this character
.go2 save den --global    ...for every character
.go2 save den=228,current ...and these rooms; the nearest is meant
.go2 delete den           forget it (--global: everyone's)
.go2 stop                 stop walking
.go2 coverage             how much of the map Hydra walks yet
.go2 confluence           into the Elemental Confluence, by an instability
                          of your element (-hot, -cold: on to that half;
                          .go2 instability: to the instability only;
                          --instability=<room>: by that one)
.route2 bank              show the way, and send nothing; on the minimap too
.route2 bank --brief      ...in a line: what a click on the minimap asks
.route2 clear             take it off the minimap
.sail kraken              make your ship ready and sail it to a port
.sail route kraken        the course there on the chart, kept as you sail;
                          sends nothing
.sail help                the ports, and how far each is
.go2 help                 these

A command may be shortened: .go is .go2, .ro is .route2, and .sa is .sail – any start of the word of two letters or more.

A walk is stopped by .go2 stop, or by .stop, which stops everything.
