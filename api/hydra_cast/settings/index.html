hydra_cast

Module settings 

Source
Expand description

A character’s caster settings: the ecaster section of its settings file (hydra_session::settings_store).

  • on: whether typed spells are cast at all; off until turned on.
  • options, character-wide: channel, conserve, safety, stance, hide.
  • aliases: a name typed in place of a spell number.
  • verbs and stances, by spell number or by alias: how that spell, or that alias of it, is cast; an alias’s own wins over its spell’s.
  • hide: the spells to hide after, while the hide option is on.

Each change says what it did, in the words the player is told.
