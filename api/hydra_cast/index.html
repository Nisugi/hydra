Crate hydra_cast 

Source
Expand description

Typed spell casting: with a character’s caster on, a spell number or one of its spell aliases typed, with a target and a count if wanted, becomes a cast.

901                  incant 901
901 kobold           prepare 901, then cast kobold
901 second kobold 3  prepare 901, then cast second kobold 3
zap kobold           the same, with `zap` an alias of 901

What a cast does is worked out here, from the line, the character’s settings (settings), the spell table and what the character is and has (plan); the game’s answers are read by answers.

  • request: a typed line as a cast asked for, or not.
  • plan: the checks a cast passes or is refused by, its stance, its verb, and the commands it sends.
  • answers: what the game says to a prepare, a cast, a release and a stance, as each needs to know.

A character’s caster, running: the typed casts it takes, one at a time in the order typed, each worked out (crate::plan) when its turn comes and then cast, reading the game’s answers as it goes.
