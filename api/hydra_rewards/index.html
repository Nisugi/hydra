Crate hydra_rewards 

Source
Expand description

Login rewards, recorded passively: nothing is ever sent to the game.

On a character’s first login of the game’s day (midnight Eastern) the game says two lines: it thanks the player for logging in, with the streak of consecutive days when there is one, and it names the reward. This crate hears them, keeps one row a character a day in the ledger, and answers whether today’s reward is in.

  • parse: the two lines, pure.
  • watcher: lines in, a reward heard out; the live recorder and the import both use it.
  • catalog: the reports named, for a window that lists them.
  • claim: a login made for the reward alone, read off the game’s bytes.
  • schedule: when a claim may be made, and whose, from rewards.toml.
  • store: when an account’s store is due a visit for the month’s Simucoins.
  • recorder: the task on a session that hears and writes.
  • replay: a recording’s rewards, for the import.
  • report: lines from rows, and .rewards.
