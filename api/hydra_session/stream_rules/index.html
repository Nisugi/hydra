Module stream_rules 

Source
Expand description

Stream rules: which stream a line of the game’s belongs to, as the player sorts them, beside the one the game sent it to. One list for every character, FILE in the data folder; the first rule that matches a line sorts it.

A rule matches a line by its words or a regular expression, and by what Hydra knows of it: that it came in a chunk the combat reader found fighting in, that the reader marked it (a flare, a status landing on the character, a killing blow), or that the bestiary knows it as one of a creature’s messages of the kinds named (its idle flavour, its arrival, its death…). A rule that names a stream looks only at the lines the game sent there. Every condition a rule gives must hold.

A line a rule matches is logged under its stream after the one the game sent it to, main/ambients: where it came from, and where the player put it. The windows are told it too (crate::Event::Sorted): it goes to its stream’s windows, and leaves the stream the game sent it to unless the rule copies it.

With no file, the rules are Rules::default: the two built in, every main line of a chunk with fighting in it to combat, and every main line of a chunk with a search or a skin in it to loot, each kept in main too. A file is the whole list: the built-in rules are in it to edit, or not. A file read before the loot rule was built in is given it once (Rules::parse).

A rule can be made from a line the player points at (Rule::like): its words as they are, with the names in it and its numbers loosened, so it takes the lines like it.

A command goes with its chunk to the stream of the rule that takes fights (Rules::command): a script’s attack, by its first word (FIGHT_COMMANDS), when the chunk it went out in had fighting in it; a stance or a target, whoever sent it (SETUP_COMMANDS), while a hostile creature is in the room, and the chunk it went out in with it. The player’s own attacks stay where they were typed. A search or a skin, whoever sent it (LOOT_COMMANDS), goes with its chunk to the rule that takes loot. A command is logged as it was sent, whatever its stream.

The log’s past lines are sorted again as they are read back (Compiled::sort_past): a line written before a rule that takes it was made is shown in its stream, though its file says otherwise.

COMBAT

The first words of a script’s commands that go with their fight, as the rule that takes fights has them when it names none: the attacks, the combat skills, the spells cast. A spell number alone is incant’s.
FILE
The rules’ file, in the data folder.
LOOT
The stream the built-in rule sorts searches and skins into.
LOOT_COMMANDS
The first words of the commands that go with their search or skin, as the rule that takes loot has them when it names none.
SETUP_COMMANDS
The first words of the commands that take their chunk with them while a hostile creature is in the room, as the rule that takes fights has them when it names none.

Functions§

fought
Whether a prompt’s chunk had fighting in it: an attack, or a fact of one – a status, a death, a crit’s stun. A spell wearing off is no fighting, nor is a change in what is worn or up.
like
A regular expression for the lines like text: its words as they are, each of names – where it names a creature, a player or a thing, by byte – any name, the article before one with it, and each number any number. Anchored at both ends, so it takes whole lines. A line with no words left but names is matched as it is.
ours
Whose attacks a prompt’s chunk had in it: Some(true), one of the character’s own; Some(false), only others’ – a creature’s, another player’s, a lasting effect’s tick no cast owns; None, no attack, or none the reader could say whose.
