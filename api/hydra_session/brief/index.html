hydra_session

Module brief 

Source
Expand description

A fight’s chunk told briefly: who attacked, then for each target what it took and how it was left, in the place of every line the game said.

The block is the attack’s first line, then for each target its rolls and its flares, the damage it took on the line that ended it ( .. 45 damage! The blow shatters ...), and its state after (A troll is KILLED!). Lines are given to a target as the game names it: a line that links a creature other than the attacker turns to it, and what the game indents after it – what each blow did – is its until another is named. A line before the first that names a target is kept as it is, before the block.

The combat reader says who attacked (Attacker) and who died: a killing blow, or a death the room confirmed; the bestiary knows a creature’s death message. The rest is read off the lines as the game words them. A frontend chooses for each kind of attacker whether the story shows the game’s lines, the block, or neither, and whether a block shows its rolls and its flares; the combat stream always has the game’s lines.
