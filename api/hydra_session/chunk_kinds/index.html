Module chunk_kinds 

Source
Expand description

The chunk kinds: which kind of chunk each sentence the game says is seen in, learned from the chunks the readers know, so a chunk they do not know can be sorted by what its sentences have been.

A chunk is the game’s answer to one thing (crate::player_log::feed::Cause). The combat reader knows a fight, and the loot reader a search or a skin; a chunk answering a command that is neither is known to be something else, and so is one that tells a room. Each sentence of a chunk known is counted under its kind (Kind); a chunk no reader knows is judged by its sentences (Table::judge) and is not counted. A sentence is the lookout’s ([hydra_lookout::key::sentence]): each object a placeholder, digits and spaces dropped.

A sentence seen STRICT_SEEN times and in one kind’s chunks STRICT_SHARE of them is that kind’s for sure: a chunk with one moves where that kind’s chunks go. Seen LOOSE_SEEN times and LOOSE_SHARE of them, it is likely that kind’s: a chunk with one is copied there, and stays in the story too. A sentence sure or likely to be something else keeps its chunk where it is, whatever else the chunk holds.

The player’s own word wins over what was counted: a line marked Part of this fight or Not part of this fight puts its sentence in that kind for sure (Mark), kept in MARKS, a file to read. The counts are kept in a file of their own for each game (path), hashes only, as the lookout’s are, and can be learned again from the recordings at any time (learn); the marks are never learned over.
