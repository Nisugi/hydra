The creature codex, read for the player: what .bestiary answers, and what the Bestiary widget shows.

The codex is the model’s bestiary (hydra_model::state::creature): 627 creature templates with where each is found, how it attacks and defends, what it drops, and what players have written about it. Nothing here watches the game: the answers are the same for everyone.

<name> is one creature’s entry: its name as typed, else the one creature of that noun, else the one whose name holds the words; several, and they are listed to choose from. here lists what spawns around the character’s room, level, area, family, undead and search the rest. Every list and entry is a linked table (crate::notice::Span): a name opens its entry, a level its level, a family its family, a ground’s [go2] walks there, [map] tints its grounds on the Map Explorer, and the wiki a page.
