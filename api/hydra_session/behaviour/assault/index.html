Module assault 

Source
Expand description

<assault>, sent as weapon <assault>: a weapon assault, whose strikes come a few at a time over several seconds, each round with its own roundtime. The chain goes on when the assault is over, not when it begins.

  1. Not usable – not known, not affordable, cooling down unless a buff waives it, overexerted – the chain stops and says why, and nothing is sent. What Hydra does not yet know does not stop it.
  2. Roundtime and cast roundtime are waited out.
  3. Sent, the lines are heard until one ends it (assaults.tsv): its own last line, or one that ends any assault, the chain going on; a refusal, the chain stopping with the game’s words. The roundtime after each round of strikes ends nothing.
  4. ...wait: waited out and sent again, at most SENT_AGAIN times. You don't seem to be able to move to do that.: sent once more if the character can move again within ten seconds.
  5. Only the chain waits. Every other command – typed, another macro, a click, Lich – goes to the game as ever, and the game decides whether it ends the assault; the chain hears the line it says.
  6. Written weapon barrage, an assault is the command as written, none of this done.
  7. .stop stops the wait with the chain. No end in HEARD_FOR, the chain stops and says so.
