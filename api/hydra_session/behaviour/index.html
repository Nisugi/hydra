Behaviour words: a chain’s piece Hydra carries out with intelligence, reading the model before it sends and the game after (crate::steps::BEHAVIOURS).

§stance <x>

  1. Already in the stance asked for: nothing sent.
  2. In roundtime: it is waited for, since the game takes no command then.
  3. Otherwise it is sent at once, a cast roundtime or not: asked for defensive in a cast roundtime, the game sets guarded and says why.
  4. Sent, the bar (pbarStance) is the truth: done when it shows the stance’s band, or guarded for defensive asked in a cast roundtime; said to have failed, and the chain stopped, when it does not within a few seconds. A ...wait is waited out and the stance sent again, at most heard::SENT_AGAIN times.

§<assault>

In assault: barrage #123 sent as weapon barrage #123. Written with weapon, an assault is the command as written.

<assault>, sent as weapon <assault>: a weapon assault, whose strikes come a few at a time over several seconds, each round with its own roundtime. The chain goes on when the assault is over, not when it begins.
