Module steps 

Source
Expand description

The chain language: what a macro, a key’s send and an alias target are made of, cut into steps.

Pieces are cut at each comma, carriage return or line break, and each is trimmed; an empty piece is dropped. A comma kept inside a command is written \,: 'Hello\, friend. Each piece is one of:

  • a command, sent as typed;
  • s1.5 – a wait of so many seconds;
  • rt – wait until the roundtime is over; ct – until the cast roundtime is over (both on the model’s clock);
  • a behaviour word, one of BEHAVIOURS: a command Hydra carries out with intelligence, reading the model before it sends and the game after. stance offensive sends nothing when the character already stands there.

The reading is here; the running is the session’s (run_steps).

Constants§

BEHAVIOURS
The words Hydra carries out itself, each the first word of its piece; every weapon assault’s mnemonic is one too (crate::behaviour::assault).
LONGEST_WAIT
The longest a wait may be, so a slip of the finger (s600) does not hold a character’s commands for ten minutes.
