Expand description

The facts of one chunk into the ledger’s rows.

The recorder holds a writing connection of its own and the ids it has handed out this hunt: the row of each creature by its exist id, and the last damaging attack on each, for crediting a death the room feed confirms later. Each chunk is one transaction.
