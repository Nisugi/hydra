Module analytics 

Source
Expand description

The analytics: a flare’s behaviour, a creature’s defences, HP estimates, control and blind coverage, aim and accuracy, kill efficiency, and two hunts compared. These use only recorded facts: a missing status event is not a failure, and a requested aim location is an assumption, never history.

alongside
The flares that fire with name, and how often: one count per attack they shared, so an area flare’s extra rows cannot push a share past every firing.
analytics
The analytics over a scope. location is the aim assumed, for Aim.
barkskin
Every bark cast: its absorbs grouped by the messages alone. An absorb that crumbled ends the bark; one that endured keeps it while the next comes within the cooldown, since nothing could be cast inside it.
compare
Two hunts side by side: the latest two unless named.
defense
DS, TD and UDF readings against a creature noun, by attack.
flare
One flare of ours: its proc rate per swing, its spread over targets against what was eligible, outcomes, statuses, companions, and its damage by crit rank and location.
hp
HP estimates for killed creatures: our damage, everyone’s, and the ordered damage list. arg is a noun or a hunt id.
intercepts
Every intercept together: of the creature attacks at us, how many would have hit and how many something of ours stopped; then by barrier, and by attacker.
