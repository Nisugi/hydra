The feeds and the trees: recent attacks and creatures, one creature’s attack tree, one attack’s detail, one spawn tree.

A creature is named by its exist id (the newest instance) or by its noun with an optional index, 1-based from the first seen, negative from the last. Everything is scoped to this character’s hunts.

Functions§

attack_by_name
The idx-th instance of the attack name (the latest by default), or the names recorded when name is none.
attack_detail
One attack: type, outcome, sequence, target, its rolls, damage components, statuses, flares, and its spawn tree.
creature
A creature’s attack tree: every spawn tree that touched it. No arg: the top twenty by damage. full draws the whole tree; otherwise one line per sequence, all of them rather than the first and last three.
tree
A spawn tree as a grid: the root, then every member with its flares.
