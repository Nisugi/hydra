cstats ucs: unarmed positioning over a scope. Every own jab, punch, kick and grapple is paired with the positioning the game said for it (said before its roll: the tier the strike was made at) and any follow-up it opened. A strike’s tier after is the tier of the next strike at the same creature in the same hunt.

Over the landed strikes below excellent, a tier that rose is told apart by its strike: the offered follow-up taken, or not. A rise with no follow-up taken is counted as rising without it.

The arms are Bold Brawler’s rank as each hunt’s gemstone set said it, so a test with the gem off and on splits on its own. The game’s line is kept in the status row: its tier, or the follow-up attack it named, in the row’s action.
