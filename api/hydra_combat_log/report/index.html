hydra_combat_log

Module report 

Source
Expand description

What the hunts show, as lines: queries over the ledger’s combat tables.

Every report takes the reader’s connection and the character, resolves which hunts it is over (Scope), and says what it found in fixed width. Ownership is the recorder’s word: attacks.ours, flares.ours, and the kill credit on a creature.

Constants§

ABSORBED
An inbound attack the bark absorbed: it would have hit.
BLOCKED
An inbound attack a barrier blocked before any roll: a block flare of ours on it, or the game’s own intercept outcome with no bark absorb.
BLOCK_FLARES
The barrier flares that stop an attack before any roll: the thorns, the image, and the two whose way is not yet known. A block says nothing of whether the attack would have hit.
INBOUND
A creature’s attack at us: not a nearby player’s, not an unowned tick, and not what we do to ourselves (the thorns’ recoil) or the room does (the cold), which the reader files as inbound from self and environment.
INTERCEPTED
An inbound attack something of ours took before it landed: blocked before any roll, or absorbed when it would have hit.
LANDED
An inbound attack that landed: a hit on us, or an outcome of hit, and not intercepted.
REACTIVE_FLARES
The flares that stop a creature’s attack on us, by name: bark, the image, the thorns’ block, the evanescent shield, the stone barrier.
