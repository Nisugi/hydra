Expand description

What one line says about the move that was just sent.

Variants§

§

MustUnhide

Hidden or invisible, and this way in wants the walker seen. Remedy: unhide.

§

WrongDoor

One of several doors: this was the wrong one. Remedy: the next ordinal (go door -> go second door).

§

NoSuchWay

The exit is wrong for the map: there is no such way from here. A caller may drop the exit.

§

Denied

Refused, but the exit is real: a guard, a ticket, a closed shop. Keep the exit, give up on it for now.

§

FailedRoll

A skill roll failed and may succeed next time: a climb lost, a swim pushed back, vertigo, fog. Remedy: wait out the roundtime, try again. fell is true where the failure leaves the walker on the ground.

Fields

§fell: bool

True when the message says the walker ended up on the ground.

§

Swam

The move worked, though the room did not change in the usual way: the long swims of Sailor’s Grief.

§

FailedRollHandsFull

A climb that needs the hands free as well as another try.

§

TooInjured

Too injured to climb. Remedy: Sigil of Resolve, if known.

§

NeedsClimb

go was sent and the game wants climb.

§

NeedsGo

climb was sent and the game wants go.

§

CannotDrag

Dragging someone, and it did not work.

§

HandsFull

Remedy: empty the hands, and give them back after.

§

Closed

Remedy: open it, once.

§

Wait(u32)

...wait N seconds. – roundtime, with the seconds.

§

MustStand

Remedy: stand.

§

StillRecovering

“still recovering from your recent…” Remedy: wait two seconds.

§

TypeAhead

Too many commands in flight. Remedy: wait a second.

§

Stunned

Remedy: wait for the stun to pass.

§

DiskWobbled

A floating disk would not follow. Remedy: just try again.

§

ItemAtFeet

Something of the walker’s is on the ground. Remedy: stow feet.

§

Trapped

A boltstone trap fired: stunned, knocked down.

§

Held

Held fast. Remedy: wait to regain control.

§

PitchDark

No light. The move succeeded: the walker arrived, it just cannot see the room.
