What one line said about the group.

One more signal needs no pattern here: the group emptying is <indicator id='IconJOINED' visible='n'/>, which arrives as a Frame::StatusIndicator – GameState::apply stores it AND empties the group on it (Group::emptied).

The members are cleared on that indicator and on You are not currently in a group, and replaced wholesale by the group command’s roster line. Without all three, nothing but a disband or a reconnect would empty the list, and a character dropped from a group by someone else would keep reporting its members.

§

NoGroupToDisband

§

Status(GroupStatus)

Your group status is currently open. (or closed).

§

Refused(Member)

<X>'s group status is closed: the game refusing group #<id> or join #<id>.

UNVERIFIED that the game sends the name as a link. Its sibling <X> joins <Y's> group. links the possessive name, so it is read as a link here, with or without the 's inside it, and a line with no link is not read.

§

Listed

You are leading <X>, <Y>. or You are grouped with <X>, <Y>.: the group command’s roster.

A complete list, so it REPLACES the members, rather than adding to them: anyone not named has left.

Fields

§leading: bool

You are leading: the leader is you, so it is not a link.

§members: Vec<Member>

Everyone the line named, in order.
