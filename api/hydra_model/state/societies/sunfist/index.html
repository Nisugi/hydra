The Guardians of Sunfist: 20 sigils, powered by stamina and mana.

§The simplest of the three

A sigil costs stamina, mana, or both, and both are paid on use – there is no level scaling as Voln has, and no deferred cost as the Council has. So affordable is two comparisons, both >=, with none of the asymmetry the Council module has to explain.

§Costs the game varies and no source pins down

  • Sigil of Distraction – the table holds the single-target cost; the room version’s cost is not known.
  • Sigil of Escape – 75 stamina / 15 mana for the ordinary use; the emergency version usable while stunned or bound costs more, by an unknown amount.

The wiki does not quantify either, and it calls Sigil of Intimidation’s mana cost “Variable” where the table commits to 5. All three are kept at the table’s value, with the uncertainty recorded here rather than silently baked in – a caller comparing a sigil’s cost against current stamina should treat these three as lower bounds.
