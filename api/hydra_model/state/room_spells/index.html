Module room_spells 

Source
Expand description

Spells laid on a room, as the character saw them: the ones that stay in the room they were cast in, and cannot be cast there again until they end. Which spells, and the lines each says, are a table, data/room_spells.tsv: a spell is added there, from lines seen in play.

§A row

A spell’s start line lays it on the room the character is in; a beat line, said while it lasts, says it is there; its stop line ends it; a refusal on the character’s own cast of it says one is laid there already. Its time is seconds a level of its caster and seconds besides, and a spell that acts on a beat ends on the last beat inside that time.

§MEASURED: Breeze

A strong breeze suddenly picks up and begins to blow around in different directions through the area!
A strong breeze continues to swirl through the area.        (each beat)
The strong breeze stops as suddenly as it arrived.
The spell misfires!                                          (evoked again while it blows)

A level 100’s evoke started at 00:22:20, beat every nine seconds and stopped at 00:27:17: 297 seconds, the three past the last beat of 300 lost.

§Whose, and how long

The end is known only for one the character cast itself, by its own level. One another caster laid, or seen only by its beats, has no known end: it lasts until its stop line, or at most what a level 100’s would from when it was last seen.

Each character keeps what it saw. Characters in one room see the same lines, so their records agree.
