Module creature 

Source
Expand description

The bestiary: what a creature is, and what the wire says it is doing.

Two halves that do not touch each other:

  • Creature – static game knowledge for 627 creatures, generated by tools/extract_creatures.rb. Everything a template says: levels, hit points, every attack and its strength, every defense, the room UIDs each is found in, what it drops, its abilities, and every line it prints – arrival, attack, casting, standing up, shaking off a stun, dying.
  • status::CreatureStatus – a stateless classifier over <crtrStatus>, which the wire sends continuously during a fight.

None of this data is derivable, which is why it is carried whole. Somebody cast Limb Disruption at 329 creatures to find out which have limbs.

§All of it

Every message kind and every field is kept: a comprehensive bestiary, and messages whose uses are not all apparent yet. A behavior that answers a creature’s casting preparation needs exactly the MessageKind::SpellPrep lines – 281 lines across 169 creatures – rather than a hand-written list of its own. The extractor aborts on any key it does not read, and load_problems reports any row or column this side does not.

§Not here: the live registry

Per-fight state – accumulated damage, stun estimates, amputated body parts, a room roster with eviction and housekeeping – is a stateful consumer, and lives in state/creatures.rs. What lives here is the classifier it sits on.

§The tri-state flags are measured facts, and absence is a third answer

Each flag is true, false, or unknown. For limbs, “has no limbs left!” after repeated casts is true, and a refusal on the very first cast is false. Those were measured in game, one creature at a time.

MEASURED across the 627 templates:

Flagtruefalseunknown
sleepable127158342
limbs3281298
blood320146161
boxes357106164

So Creature::sleepable is None for 342 of 627. A sorcerer asking “can I Sleep this” must be able to tell “measured, and no” from “nobody has tried” – the same three-valued discipline as PsmSet::has_table and Effects::active_in. Read as a plain bool, the treasure flags would tell a looter that 164 creatures carry no boxes when nobody had looked.

§A defense is usually a RANGE

MEASURED: melee is a range 511 times, a scalar 37 times, absent 79 times. Creature defenses vary by spawn, and Stat keeps that distinction – collapsing a range to its midpoint would report a precision the bestiary never claimed.
