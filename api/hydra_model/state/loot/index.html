What the game says of loot: a creature searched or mugged, a box opened and its coins gathered, an item appraised at a shop, an item sold. Each is one LootEvent with the objects’ ids, read off the chunk a prompt closed, and taken by whoever records them.

§A search, one chunk

You search the gigas berserker.
You discard the berserker's useless equipment.
She had 563 silvers on her.
She had a flickering snowfire ruby on her!
Interesting, she carried some dashing samite boots on her.
She had nothing else of value.

The creature is the search line’s object; each had ... on her! and carried ... on her. line’s object past the creature is an item; the silver is the had N silvers on line. didn't carry any silver and had nothing of interest are the search’s own words for nothing.

§A mug

You boldly accost a brawny gigas shield-maiden, your attack masking your larcenous intent!
Taking advantage of the scuffle, you roughly pat the gigas shield-maiden down for hidden valuables!
You rifle her pockets and discover 311 silvers!
Your hasty search scatters the gigas shield-maiden's riches to the ground!

What a mug scatters is named by nothing but the room’s objects after it.

§A box

Opening a box sends no line: a container frame titled for the box, then its contents. The event comes at the chunk’s end, with what the box holds. The coins are a line of their own:

You gather the remaining 1,576 coins from inside your maoral strongbox.

§An appraisal, and a sale

You ask Cendadric to appraise some embossed spiked veil iron leg greaves.
Cendadric turns the greaves over in his hands a few times.
He nods, eyeing the veil iron leg greaves with appreciation.  "This is quite a fine item, worth approximately 36,042 silvers.  However, since that is out of my price range, I will offer you 25,000 silvers for it."
He smiles broadly and nods.  "Not a bad little find there, Nisugi.  I'll give you 1,021 for this if you'd like."
The jeweler Etaenia takes the platinum nugget and inspects it carefully before saying, "I'll give you 2,000 silvers for it if you want to sell."
Cendadric takes your small statue, glances at it briefly, then hands you 1,021 silver coins.
The jeweler Etaenia takes the gold ingot, gives it a careful examination and hands you 4,500 silver for it.

§The locksmith pool

The attendant takes your box and says, "Your tip of 300 silvers has been recorded, and the 2,684 silver fee has been collected.  We'll get someone on that right away."
The attendant says, "Alright, here's your iron strongbox back."

The box dropped is named by its noun alone, so it is the thing a hand held last with that noun: the hand frames come before the line. The box handed back is the line’s link, a new object.

§The rare finds

You notice a scintillating mote of Gemstone Dust on the ground and gather it quickly.
 ** A glint of light catches your eye, and you notice a rough crystalline geode at your feet! **
 ** A glint of light catches your eye, and you notice a rough crystalline geode at Zoleta's feet! **
A prismatic display of color tints the air around you and arcs away, heralding your discovery of a legendary treasure!
*** A prismatic display of color arcs across the sky over the Hinterwilds, heralding the discovery of a legendary treasure! ***
*** A refracted display of prismatic color tints the air around you, heralding the distant discovery of a legendary treasure! ***
The gigas berserker turns and runs screaming into the distance, never to be seen again.
In a breathtaking display of destruction, you smash the a vibrant golden bauble to pieces.  Confetti flies everywhere!  You think you hear the sound of a trumpet fanfare.  In the confetti-strewn remnants, you discover a tiny pink vial!

Dust, a gemstone at your own feet and a legendary treasure of your own are finds off the creature searched or mugged last, which the find lines never name. A gemstone at another’s feet and a legendary treasure heralded elsewhere are witnessed, not found. A creature sent fleeing is as good as searched. A bauble smashed is what came out of it.
