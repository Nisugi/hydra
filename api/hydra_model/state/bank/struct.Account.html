pub fn local(&self, location: &str) -> Option<&BankBalance>

The bank serving a town, by the room’s location.

Three passes: an exact prefix first, then a looser containment either way, then the town’s own word in the location (Solhaven of the free port of Solhaven, for Vornavis Bank of Solhaven). A bank whose name says no town is known by TOWN_OF_BANK. An empty location matches nothing.

pub fn agrees(&self) -> Option<bool>

Whether the stated total matches the rows read.

None when the game stated no total. Some(false) means a row was missed – the condition that would hide a bank’s silver, and which a sum-based total cannot express.
