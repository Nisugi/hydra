A ship, and the ocean it sails: what the game says of them.

§A ship’s room is a room; the water it sits in is not

Aboard, <nav> names the deck the character stands on, the same number on every ship of that hull. Where the ship is the game says only in text: a second room printed in the room’s own style, on departing, on LOOK OCEAN, on PEER, and, while the ocean is watched, on the familiar stream at every move. That second room is a view (Waters). It never moves the character (Room), and the <compass> the game sends after it is the view’s, not the deck’s (Ship::holds_compass).

§What is kept

Ship::waters is the last view of where the ship is, as stated, and Ship::legs the moves the game has told of. A move with no view after it leaves the waters as they were stated: Waters::as_of against Ship::moves says how many moves old they are, and working out where that leaves the ship is for whoever holds the chart.

Views are read as their lines arrive, not at the prompt, because a view on the familiar stream belongs to no prompt’s chunk and the compass that follows one must be held before the prompt.
