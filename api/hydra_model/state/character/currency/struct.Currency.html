Expand description

Every balance a character carries.

All Option: a character who has never been to Duskruin has unknown bloodscrip, not zero. That distinction is why a consumer can tell “you have none” from “nobody has said”, and why a display can omit a currency the player has never earned rather than showing a row of zeroes.

Silver on your person, from wealth.

§silver_container: Option<u64>

Silver in a container you are carrying.

§silver_total: Option<u64>

The whole: You are carrying a total of N silver., or N silver between notes and coins.

§notes: Option<u64>

Promissory notes, not coins: Total note value: N, or wealth’s <town> promissory notes valued at a total of N silver.

§voln_favor: Option<i64>

Voln favor. Signed: the wire can send a negative.

§tickets: Option<u64>

Simucoin general tickets.

Gold (“Gold - N gold.”).

Gigas artifact fragments.

§redsteel_marks: Option<u64>

Redsteel marks.

§dust: Option<u64>

Dust, held “in your reserves”.

Named for the item, not the game. gemstone_dust would name the GAME rather than what you are carrying – and the game-name scanner flags that spelling in a shared module, correctly: the wire says only N Dust in your reserves, so dust is both the accurate name and the one that passes.
