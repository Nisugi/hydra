Body parts, and the wound/scar accessors over them.

Wounds and scars are the same shape read two ways: one type, and a Track to say which. The one real difference is which the game’s injury mode shows (InjuryMode).

§Sixteen parts, not fourteen

The game’s documentation lists fourteen body locations. The wire carries sixteen: it adds leftFoot and rightFoot, which the sneaking rule reads (injured.rs). The documentation is authoritative where it speaks and incomplete elsewhere.

§The composites are not the obvious groupings

Three of them, and two are surprising enough that the tests assert their membership rather than their results:

CompositePartsNote
armsarms and handsfour parts, not two
limbsarms, hands, legsno feet, though the wire has them
torsochest, abdomen, back and both eyeseyes are not torso anatomically

The shapes carry meaning: a rank-3 limbs scar is a severed limb and a rank-3 eye scar a missing eye, which is why limbs excluding feet matters – a severed foot is not a severed limb for that purpose.

Each takes the maximum, not a sum. A character with four rank-1 wounds has arms == 1.
