Module cost

Expand description

What a PSM costs to use, and whether it can be used now.

§The table

data/psm_costs.tsv, generated by tools/extract_psm_costs.rb: 187 rows, 11 armor, 80 combat maneuver, 33 feat, 33 shield, 24 weapon and 6 warcry, each with the line it was cut from. Every cost is one gauge and a number, stamina for all but Excoriate (10 mana), and two are two numbers: Burst of Swiftness and Surge of Strength cost 60 stamina while their own cooldown is up and 30 otherwise.

Those two are priced at the moment of the question, from the Cooldowns dialog as it stands then, never from a value fixed earlier. A cost passes only when the gauge holds strictly more than it: exactly 30 stamina does not afford a 30-stamina cost.

Two feats share the mnemonic wps (weighting and padding). Both rows are kept; they cost the same, which the tool checks, so a lookup by mnemonic does not depend on which it finds.

Read on each lookup rather than parsed once into a static: a hunt asks once a step, and 187 short lines is not worth a process global.

§The rule

GameState::psm_availability answers four tests separately, so a caller can tell why as well as whether:

testpasses whenread from
knownranks >= 1PsmSet::get; unknown until the category’s list is read
affordablecost < current points, strictlythe gauge’s current points, when the bar states them
not coolingnot in Cooldowns by nameEffects::active_named with the long name
not overexertedOverexerted not in Debuffsthe same, for Overexerted

And the waivers: a weapon or shield area_of_effect technique is affordable whatever it costs while Glorious Momentum is up, and a weapon technique ignores its cooldown while Glorious Momentum is up for an area_of_effect one, or Ardor of the Scourge for an assault.

§What is not answered

  • Warcries’ availability. warcry_cost gives the cost, but whether a warcry can be used also turns on being cutthroated or silenced, and the known warcries (Standing::warcries) are a set with no “never stated” of their own. A caller gating warcries applies its own thresholds.
  • Forced roundtime, which raises the cost by 25 + 10 x count percent.
  • Ignoring the cooldown for Burst and Surge, which a caller would ask for explicitly.
