nextLvlPB’s value=.

§gift_pulses: u32

How many times nextLvlPB’s text has changed this session: the gift pulse. A count, not minutes left: nothing on the wire says when the gift began.

PTPs: physical training points.

p2m: physical points converted to mental.

m2p: mental points converted to physical.

mindState’s field_exp=, or the report’s Field Exp: first half.

Deeds:.

Exp until lvl:, below the level cap.

§until_training_point: Option<u32>

Exp to next TP:, at the level cap.

§report_training: Option<(u32, u32)>

The report’s PTPs/MTPs:, at the level cap: physical then mental.

§converted_training: Option<u32>

The skills table’s footer: physical training points converted to mental, (2866 Phy converted to Mnt); Some(0) when the footer says none were.

§until_ascension_point: Option<u32>

Exp to next ATP:: experience to the next ascension point.

§ascension_points: Option<u32>

ATPs:: ascension points held.

§instant_absorbed: u64

Experience absorbed at once since login, summed from every [You have instantly absorbed N experience points!]: a brooch rubbed, a bounty turned in on a full field pool, books handed in. A reader that watches it rise knows that much of the next gain was no pulse.

§instant_count: u32

How many such lines came.

§brooch_rubs: u32

Brooch rubs since login: You trace the central orb of ... in your brooch, which an instant absorb follows.
