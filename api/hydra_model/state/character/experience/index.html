Experience and encumbrance: the expr and encum dialogs, and the experience command’s report.

expr:  <label id='yourLvl' value='Level 100'/>
       <progressBar id='mindState' value='100' text='saturated' field_exp='1445'
         max_field_exp='1403' ascension_exp='24158344' fashlonae='2' exp='43904921'
         until_next='79'/>
       <progressBar id='nextLvlPB' value='100' text='43904921 experience'/>
       <label id='PTPs' value='3709 PTPs'/> <label id='MTPs' value='217 MTPs'/>
       <label id='p2m' value='2122 P2M'/> <label id='m2p' value='0 M2P'/>
encum: <progressBar id='encumlevel' value='0' text='None'/>
       <label id='encumblurb' value='You are not encumbered enough to notice.'/>

The experience report, two facts a line:

          Level: 100                         Fame: 140,102,815
     Experience: 12,736,530             Field Exp: 0/990
  Ascension Exp: 0                  Recent Deaths: 1
      Total Exp: 12,736,530         Death's Sting: None
  Long-Term Exp: 0                          Deeds: 6
 Exp to next TP: 970
      PTPs/MTPs: 428/0

Below the level cap the last lines are Exp until lvl: instead; at it, Exp to next TP: and PTPs/MTPs:, and with ascension Exp to next ATP: and ATPs: beside them. Every line is kept, including the ones that look disposable.
