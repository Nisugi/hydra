Creatures that hide, and the ones the game forgets to give back.

When a creature hides it is removed from the room’s creatures. If it is seen to hide, or it disappears without dying and without being seen to leave, it hid. And when a creature attacks from hiding, the game sometimes fails to add it back to the room’s creatures, so a script cannot target it. This module watches for the attack and puts the creature back.

Two mechanisms, and the second is the one that earns the module:

What it doesWhy
Hidingremembers something is hidden hereso a behavior can reveal it before leaving
Revealputs the creature back on the rostera game bug drops it, and a script cannot target what is not there

§A creature can hide without saying so

The prose is not the only signal, and it is not the reliable one. A creature that vanishes from room creatures without dying and without being seen to leave has hidden — that is an inference the roster already supports, because Creatures keeps the previous roster and flags the dead (Classification::Dead).

That inference does not live here. It is Creatures::vanished_unaccounted, on the roster that owns the facts it reasons over: one home per fact.

§Patterns are prose; participants are links

Every hiding and reveal line names its creature the same way:

<pushBold/>a <a exist="123" noun="troll">troll</a><popBold/> slips into hiding.

The parser already yields both the link and the bold depth, so a rule here is the prose alone and the participants are the links. No pattern re-tokenizes that fragment.

§Monsterbold is the creature test

A reveal requires bold and a positive exist id, deliberately: this re-targets hidden creatures, and a player stepping out of hiding is not a hunt target.

VERIFIED against the corpus: every line of these shapes from a player has a negative id and no bold, and none of them is read as a reveal:

<a exist="-10319971" noun="Riend">Riend</a> comes out of hiding.
<a exist="-11182674" noun="Clairebie">Clairebie</a> leaps from hiding to attack!

Players hiding are tracked separately, by the room players status that RoomPlayer::is_hiding reads, and this must not overwrite it.

§Per-creature messages collapse

Each creature has its own reveal and strike wording; they collapse into the shared prose below.
