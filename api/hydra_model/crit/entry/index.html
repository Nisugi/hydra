CritEntry: one row of one critical-hit table.

The enums in crit/types.rs are the vocabulary; this is the record built out of them.

The source tables give the damage type and location twice – once as a table key and once inside the record. The extractor asserts the two agree for all 2,394 entries and keeps one copy, in damage_type and location.
