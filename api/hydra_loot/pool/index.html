A box dropped at the locksmith pool and the box handed back, linked.

The box goes in as one object and comes back as another. The attendant names it both times, in full when it is quoted (a rune-incised iron strongbox) and short when it is handed back (here's your iron strongbox back), so the link is by the name’s last two words, its material and its noun, and by order among the boxes of that name waiting. Two boxes of one short name in the pool at once can come back in either order, so the link says when it was ambiguous.

You want a locksmith to open a rune-incised iron strongbox for a tip of 219 silvers, or 3 percent of the box value.  There is also a fee of 2,716 silvers due up front, totaling 2,935 silvers.  You will need that amount on you or in a local bank.
The attendant takes your strongbox and says, "Your tip of 300 silvers has been recorded, and the 2,536 silver fee has been collected.  We'll get someone on that right away."
The attendant says, "Alright, here's your iron strongbox back."
