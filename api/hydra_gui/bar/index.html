Module bar 

Source
Expand description

A progress bar: it fills across or upright (or as an orb, a ring, or a panel under its stat’s emblem), its height and width adjustable, with an overlay laid over it, and text inside it, outside it on any of the four sides, or none – the text numbers, a percent, a label, or any mix of them.

An overlay may be a skin’s frame painted over the bar, a nine-slice (Overlay::framed). Unlike egui’s own bar, an empty bar paints no sliver of fill at zero.

Modules§

stack
The vitals stack: a spirit orb in the centre, with health, mana and stamina rings round it, stacked outward, Hydra sizing and centring them in the square it is given. Fills and tracks only: no rims, no kits, no words inside, so the rings read at a glance; the words are said to a screen reader.
vessel
A vessel: an orb drawn as an hourglass, a flask or a lantern. The vessel’s picture lies on top; under it, its inside – the fill seen through the vessel’s mask, the glass that shows – faint as a ghost, and below the level in the bar’s colour. No glass goes under a vessel.

Structs§

Amount
A bar’s value: its percent, and its numbers when the game gave them.
Bar
A progress bar: ui.add(Bar::new("HP", Some(amount)).fill(HEALTH)).
Kit
A bar or ring overlay kit: looping rails, and a cap for each end of a bar, drawn at the scale the bar’s thickness makes of the kit’s height. The fill and its track keep to the channel: rail pixels in from each long side and, on a bar, cap pixels in from each end, at that scale. A cap picture may be wider than cap: its ornament reaches over the channel. A ring has no caps.
Look
How a bar widget draws its bar: which way it fills, where its text goes, what the text says, its colour, and an image laid over it. The player picks it on the widget’s own page in the settings menu, which its right-click opens, and it is kept with the layout.
Overlay
Art laid over a bar: a texture of size pixels, stretched whole, or framed – a nine-slice, whose corners keep their size and whose edges stretch only along the bar, with the centre left clear so the fill shows through, as a skin’s frame lies over a bar.
Says
What a bar’s text says, in this order: its label, the game’s word for its state, its numbers (current/max), its percent. Any combination; none is no text.
Threshold
From from percent up, a bar fills in color, until a higher threshold takes over.
