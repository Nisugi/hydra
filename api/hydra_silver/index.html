Crate hydra_silver 

Source
Expand description

Silver and bounty points, recorded passively: nothing is ever sent to the game.

The bank’s teller states every deposit and withdrawal, a note’s surcharge, and a locksmith his fee; the bounty office states the points a task earned. This crate hears those lines, keeps one row each in the ledger, and sums a day’s on request. A note withdrawal names no amount, so the withdraw line typed just before gives it. Silver found on a search or a mug is stated only to whoever did it, and the pile it leaves is followed by its id to whichever character of the household picks it up.

  • parse: the lines, pure.
  • watcher: lines in, transactions heard out.
  • piles: coin piles on the ground by id, shared by every character, so a pile one character dropped and another picked up is one fact.
  • catalog: the reports named, for a window that lists them.
  • recorder: the task on a session that hears and writes.
  • report: lines from rows, and .silver.
