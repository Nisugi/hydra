The lines that state silver moving or bounty points earned, read.

The bank’s teller, on a deposit of coins or notes:

You deposit 7,800 silvers into your account.  The teller carefully records the transaction and says, "Thank you, Sugiin.  This brings your total to 8,096,946 silvers.  Have a nice day!"
You hand your notes to the teller, who glances over each one and scribbles the amounts in a book.  She says, "They add up to 112,584 silvers, bringing your account balance up to 622,900,288."
You hand over your notes to the teller, who nods, carefully recording each amount.  She says, "That's a total of 15,331 silvers, bringing your balance to 637,882 silvers."

On a withdrawal of coins, or of a note, whose amount the line does not say:

The teller scribbles the transaction into a book and hands you 1,909 silvers.
The teller carefully records the transaction, hands you 8,000 silvers, and says, "This brings your total to 8,089,146 silvers."
The teller taps her quill thoughtfully on the counter and says, "I'll have to give you a Mist Harbor promissory note for that amount."  She scribbles something onto a blank note and hands it to you, saying, "There's a 0 silver surcharge in addition to that.  Your remaining balance is 620,394,399.  Have fun spending it!"
The teller purses her lips and says, "Hmm, we will have to give you a Northwatch bond note for that amount."  The teller makes some marks on a blank note and hands it to you, saying "That is a 50 silver surcharge.  Have a nice day."

Silver found: a search or a mug of your own, the fossil charm’s flies, a box’s coins gathered, and a bounty’s reward, which also pays points:

He had 686 silvers on him.
You rifle her pockets and discover 311 silvers!
You summon a swarm of corpse flies from your silvery fossil charm, which takes off in search of silvers.  They locate a stack of 153 coins on the ground, reclaiming them and returning to you.  The corpse flies retreat back inside the charm.
You gather the remaining 412 coins.
[You have earned 1609 bounty points, 770 experience points, and 1983 silver.]

Silver from a sale, the shop named; silver paid for a purchase or handed to someone; a locksmith’s attendant, on a box left in the pool:

Cendadric takes your small statue, glances at it briefly, then hands you 1,021 silver coins.
The jeweler Etaenia takes the gold ingot, gives it a careful examination and hands you 4,500 silver for it.
The jeweler Etaenia removes the gems and hands you a Mist Harbor promissory note for 25,312 silvers.
The jeweler Etaenia takes the leather satchel, inspects the contents carefully, and removes the gems she is interested in.  She hands it back to you, along with 7,250 silver.
Poxy Pell takes the 2,000 silvers you offer in payment.
You hand Kilvath 500 silvers.
The attendant takes your strongbox and says, "Your tip of 300 silvers has been recorded, and the 2,536 silver fee has been collected.  We'll get someone on that right away."

Only your own lines are read: another character’s search or charm is told without an amount, or as theirs, and is theirs to record.

Structs§

Stated
What one line stated: a kind, its amount, and who was on the other side when the line names them. A line can state two (a tip and a fee; a bounty’s points and its silver).

Constants§

CHARM
The party named for silver the fossil charm’s flies brought.

Functions§

is_hand_pickup
Whether line is a pile of coins picked up by hand, which states no amount: the pile’s id, linked in the line, says whose it was.
is_note_withdrawal
Whether line is a note withdrawal: the teller writing a note for the amount asked, which the line never states.
is_own_gather
Whether line is the searcher’s own pile gathered as the search ends (You gather the remaining 412 coins.): the search stated the silver already, so the line is the pile taken and no more.
line
What a teller’s, a shop’s, an attendant’s or the bounty office’s line stated; nothing for any other line. A note withdrawal states only its surcharge: its amount is the withdraw typed, withdraw_typed.
withdraw_typed
The amount a typed withdraw line asks for: withdraw 5139, WITHDRAW 10,000 silvers. None for any other line.
