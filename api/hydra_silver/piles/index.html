Module piles 

Source
Expand description

Coin piles on the ground, by their id, shared by every character’s watcher in the process.

A search or a mug states its silver only to the one who did it, and the pile it drops gets one id everyone in the room sees. When another character of the household picks that pile up, by hand or by the fossil charm, the pile’s birth says whose it was and how much it held. A pile nobody here dropped is unknown, and the picker’s own line has to say what it held, if it does.
