Apple’s push service: one alert to one phone, over HTTP/2.

What Apple is handed says only “Hydra”: the alert itself goes sealed, under sealed, and mutable-content asks the phone to let HydraFE’s notification extension open it before the banner shows. A phone that cannot open it shows the words here.
