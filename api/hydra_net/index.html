hydra-net: the bottom layer.

The pipe a session’s bytes come through, the eAccess login that opens it, and the record of what went through it. Nothing here knows what a Frame is.

  • eaccess: account login (docs/eaccess-spec.md), producing a LaunchPayload.
  • live: the game connection (LiveSource), with keepalive and the WebSocket shim fallback when the game port is unreachable.
  • bytes: the ByteSource trait every source implements.
  • replay, record: replaying recorded bytes, and recording them.
  • sink: writes a session’s raw wire to <char>-<stamp>.bytes plus a small .log.
  • answering: a stand-in game for tests in this and higher crates.
  • eastern: the US Eastern clock the game’s own day runs on.
  • trainer: the play.net skill manager, read and answered without a browser.
