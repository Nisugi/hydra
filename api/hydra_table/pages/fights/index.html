Module fights 

Source
Expand description

The Fights pages: how the story shows fights, for every character (fights.toml, and the stream rules for keeping fights and loot) and for one, over every character’s (the fights section of its settings file).

A character’s page shows each choice as it is in effect, and says when it is every character’s: a choice made there is the character’s own until it is put back.
