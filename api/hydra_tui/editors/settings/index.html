hydra_tui::editors

Module settings 

Source
Expand description

The settings, over a terminal’s screen: Hydra’s own pages, and, over a character’s screen, that character’s pages from the binary, each drawn as a form (form.rs); and the keys, which open their own editor.

The pages are listed down the left. Up and Down choose one; Enter or Tab goes into it, and Escape or Tab comes back out to the list. A change to Hydra’s pages is made here, at once; a character’s is asked of the binary, which makes it through the writer that page’s file has and gives the pages again. What a change did is said at the foot.
