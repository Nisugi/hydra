hydra_tui

Module editors 

Source
Expand description

The editors over a terminal’s screen, each an overlay of its own (the main menu opens them): the settings, the highlights, the hotbars, the theme and the keys; and one window of a character’s layout (.editwindow).

Modules§

highlights
The highlights editor, over a terminal’s screen: every highlight listed with its category and what it looks for, narrowed by a filter, and one in a form to change, or a new one. A change is made at once to every story’s highlights and to the file with the next batch of saves, as the windows’ editor makes it; one refused says why.
hotbars
The hotbars editor, over a terminal’s screen: over a character’s, its bars – every character’s with its own over them, each marked whose – and over the hub’s, every character’s alone. A bar’s name, whose it is, its grid and its buttons; a button is a slot holding a macro, by its name, which says what the button shows and sends. A bar is saved whole to the file it is kept in, the rest of that file as it was, and written at once, so a key pressing it sends what was saved.
keys
The keys editor, over a terminal’s screen: every key a macro set binds, Hydra’s own among them in set 0, each with what it does, whose file says so, and Hydra’s default. Opened over a character’s screen it is that character’s keys, its own file over every character’s; over the hub’s, every character’s alone.
settings
The settings, over a terminal’s screen: Hydra’s own pages, and, over a character’s screen, that character’s pages from the binary, each drawn as a form (form.rs); and the keys, which open their own editor.
theme
The theme, over a terminal’s screen: every theme there is, built in and in the themes folder, the one marked shown in its own colours – a line of the game’s text on its ground, and a swatch of each main colour – before it is worn.
window
One window of a character’s layout edited as a form (.editwindow, or e while arranging): its place and least and most sizes, its frame, its title, its colours, whether it is locked or shown, and every setting of its widget’s own that the layout file holds as a single value.
