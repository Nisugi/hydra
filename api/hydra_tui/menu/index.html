Module menu 

Source
Expand description

A menu over the screen: a game object’s menu, or one of Hydra’s own. Its entries are chosen with the arrows and Enter or a click; Escape or a click elsewhere closes it. An entry that cannot be chosen is shown dim, and the arrows pass it by; a heading names the entries under it.

An entry may hold a menu of its own, marked >: the mouse over it, a click, Right or Enter opens it beside it, and its entries take the keys; Left or Escape closes it again, and the mouse over another entry of the menu it is in. Menus open within menus so, as deep as they go.

A menu made to find in (Menu::finding) takes what is typed: the entries whose words hold it, from every menu within, listed flat under the names of the menus they are in; Backspace takes a letter back.
