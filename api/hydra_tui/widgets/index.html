Module widgets 

Source
Expand description

Each widget of the catalog, drawn in a terminal’s cells: the story and the streams as text that scrolls; the vitals and the game’s other gauges as bars by eighths of a cell; the clocks counting down; the hands, the room and its lists as text whose links do what the windows’ do; the compass as a grid of arrows; an indicator lit in its colour; the minimap in braille dots; the injuries as a figure coloured part by part.

A widget taller than its window scrolls with the wheel, its rows below counted at the window’s foot. What a widget keeps between frames – a container opened, the page of its own it shows, the minimap’s zoom – is kept by its window’s tab (States). Its buttons are pressed by a click (press): a line sent as typed, a page turned, a question asked over the screen.

A widget following another character draws that character’s state, its name before what it says, and its links only show: what they would send is not this character’s to send.
