Module relay

Expand description

Hydra’s side of an attached terminal: two threads of its own.

One reads what the terminal sends. The other keeps the terminal’s screen: woken by what the terminal did and by the board (each character’s feed wakes it as it wakes the windows), it draws the screen into bytes and sends them. What the terminal did is drawn at once; what the game did, no oftener than PACE; the clocks at the next whole second, or oftener in roundtime. A screen that cannot be written to for WRITE_WAIT is given up, as is one whose terminal never says hello.

Its characters play on when it leaves: a terminal is a window, not a session.
