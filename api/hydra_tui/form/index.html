Module form 

Source
Expand description

A page of settings as a form: each row as its kind draws it (hydra_ui::settings), changed with the keys.

Up and Down mark a row; a heading is passed by. On the row marked:

  • a toggle switches with Space or Enter;
  • a choice steps with Left and Right, or Enter lists its choices;
  • a whole number or a number steps with Left and Right (ten steps with Shift), Enter applying it, or is typed;
  • a slider and a time of day change as they step, so what they set is seen at once; a time of day is typed too, 14:15;
  • words, a list, a colour (#rrggbb) and a font are typed: Enter, or a character typed, edits; Enter applies, Escape drops what was typed;
  • a row of buttons marks one with Left and Right, Space switching it;
  • a map and a table are shown, and changed with the commands their help names.

Delete puts a setting the page’s file holds back to its default. What is typed that is not of its kind is refused here, before anything is asked, and why is said under the rows.
