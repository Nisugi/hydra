Expand description

A hub card, in a terminal: the design every character’s card is drawn from (hydra_board::card, the windows’ cards editor’s), its cells scaled to the terminal’s.

The design is laid out in points; a terminal has cells. A column is COL points wide and a row ROW points tall, a line of the windows’ text, so a card keeps its shape; no card is smaller than NARROWEST by SHORTEST. A terminal’s letters are wider than the windows’, so each part is given at least the columns its words take, and parts beside one another keep their order and a cell between them, those after pushed along and the card widened (layout). Each cell’s widget draws as it does on a character’s screen, from the character’s state and story; the card’s own parts are the hub’s: the name and how it is connected, where it is and who with, and its buttons. A Field says one value of the game state, read from a print of it kept a short while for every field of the card. The four vitals say their label and percent over their bar: a card is small.

Functions§

draw
Draw seat‘s card from design in inside, the card’s inside – its nickname on the roster, when it has one, after its name; its files in the data folder data: each cell’s widget in its scaled place, cut to the card; the buttons’ and the links’ places kept in hits.
layout
Each of design‘s cells’ place, from the card’s inside’s top left – none for a cell that shows nothing – and the card’s inside, in columns and rows: each cell as scaled, at least as wide as its words (needs), and pushed along past those before it on its rows, a cell between them at the least; the inside the design’s, as wide as its cells reach, never smaller than NARROWEST by SHORTEST.
scaled
A cell’s rect of the design – x, y, width, height in points – as cells from the card’s inside’s top left: its edges each put on the nearest cell, a column and a row at the least.
size
The card’s inside, in columns and rows, as layout has it.
stages
How quick seat‘s terminal and session are, drawn in area under its card while Performance is on: each stage’s median and slow end over its last samples, coloured by how they read, how many there are, and how often the screen fell behind. The stages a screen’s drawing takes are a terminal’s, apart from the windows’.
