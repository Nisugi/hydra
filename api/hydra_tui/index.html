Crate hydra_tui

Expand description

Hydra in a terminal.

The screen is drawn inside Hydra’s own process, from the same board the windows and the web page draw from, and sent to whichever terminal attached: hydra-tui in any terminal on this computer, or over SSH to it. Several terminals can attach at once, each its own screen; closing one leaves its characters playing.

relay is Hydra’s side: a screen kept for each terminal. client is the terminal’s: its keys and mouse sent, the frames drawn written. wire is what they say to each other.

Modules§

app
One terminal’s screen: the hub, or a character’s; the bar at the foot that names every character and goes between them; and a menu over either when one is open.
arrange
Arranging a character’s screen: its windows moved, sized, added, taken away, their tabs and frames changed, by keys or by the mouse.
ask
A terminal asking the Hydra running on a data folder for its screen.
backend
A screen drawn into bytes: the escape sequences a terminal writes to show it, gathered for the wire rather than written to a console.
card
A hub card, in a terminal: the design every character’s card is drawn from (hydra_board::card, the windows’ cards editor’s), its cells scaled to the terminal’s.
chrome
A window’s chrome as its layout sets it: its border on the sides it has, in its style and colour, over its background, and its title where it sits on the top or bottom edge, the border drawn behind it.
client
The terminal’s side of hydra-tui: the terminal made raw and given to Hydra, which draws on it.
client_keys
A terminal’s key events named as the keys file names keys: by the physical key, KeyA, Digit1, Numpad8, ArrowUp, F3, with the modifiers held, and what the key types besides.
edit
A line of text being typed: the command line, and every field of a form.
editors
The editors over a terminal’s screen, each an overlay of its own (the main menu opens them): the settings, the highlights, the hotbars, the theme and the keys; and one window of a character’s layout (.editwindow).
find
Finding words in a window of text: Ctrl+F, then F3 and Shift+F3 for the next and the one before.
form
A page of settings as a form: each row as its kind draws it (hydra_ui::settings), changed with the keys.
hits
What a click finds: each frame drawn says where its links, buttons and tabs are, and a click is looked up among them, the last drawn on top.
hub
The hub, in a terminal: every character Hydra runs, at a glance, in four tabs – Live, the characters playing, each a card drawn from the card design (card.rs), the shared streams’ chat below them; Offline, those that ended this run, then the roster’s characters not playing, to start; Teams, roster characters started together; and New login, a login by account.
keys
The keys, as the windows have them: Hydra’s defaults, every character’s keybinds file over them, and a character’s own file over that, in the macro set it has chosen (hydra_board::keys). A key pressed on a character’s screen does what its binding says before anything else sees it; one bound to nothing types, or moves the line, as keys do.
layout
A character’s screen in a terminal, laid out in Vellum’s layout format: a Vellum layout.toml reads as it is, and Hydra’s is one.
logs
A character’s log, read in a terminal: its newest lines over the screen, the newest at the foot, each with its time and the window it was said in; scrolled with Page Up and Page Down, the arrows and the wheel; and narrowed, as the find line is typed, to the lines holding what it holds. F5 reads it again.
menu
A menu over the screen: a game object’s menu, or one of Hydra’s own. Its entries are chosen with the arrows and Enter or a click; Escape or a click elsewhere closes it. An entry that cannot be chosen is shown dim, and the arrows pass it by; a heading names the entries under it.
opens
What each of the main menu’s entries opens: the web page’s switch and its address and code, Shut down’s question, and the editors, each an overlay of its own.
overlay
What shows over a terminal’s screen and takes its keys while it does: a form, an editor, a list to choose from, the main menu. They stack: the one on top has the keys and the mouse, and closing it shows the one beneath. Escape closes the one on top unless it says otherwise.
relay
Hydra’s side of an attached terminal: two threads of its own.
seat
One character’s screen: its layout’s windows, and its command line at the foot. What was typed is sent as the windows send it; the keys do what the keybinds say (keys.rs), Hydra’s own by default: the arrows walk the history, Page Up and Page Down scroll the window in use, Tab finishes what is typed or targets the next creature.
select
Text chosen with the mouse in a window of text, and copied.
story
A character’s story as a terminal shows it: each line the game sent, a line typed after its prompt, Hydra’s answers, in the colours the windows give them; which lines are drawn decided as the windows decide (hydra_ui::lines): a prompt again with nothing between left out, a stream’s line left to its own window when one is open.
text
Text as a window shows it: lines of styled pieces, wrapped at words to the window’s width, the newest at the foot; scrolled back by whole lines, held where they were while new ones come in below; each link’s cells kept for a click to find.
theme
The theme’s colours as a terminal draws them: each meaning’s colour (Token) as 24-bit colour, and a run of the game’s text in the colour the windows give it.
vellum
The terminal’s widgets for the windows of a Vellum layout: each a struct filled with what it shows and drawn into its area. They are filled from a character’s state (feed.rs, lists.rs, panels.rs, text.rs), and each window’s frame is drawn once (crate::chrome), so a widget here draws its content alone.
widgets
Each widget of the catalog, drawn in a terminal’s cells: the story and the streams as text that scrolls; the vitals and the game’s other gauges as bars by eighths of a cell; the clocks counting down; the hands, the room and its lists as text whose links do what the windows’ do; the compass as a grid of arrows; an indicator lit in its colour; the minimap in braille dots; the injuries as a figure coloured part by part.
wire
What a terminal and Hydra say to each other over the instance’s loopback port, once the terminal has asked for a screen (hydra-tui).
work
Work mode: the terminal’s screen covered, so that a glance at it shows a terminal at work and nothing of the game.
