hydra_tui::vellum

Module lists 

Source
Expand description

The lists and the room, filled from a character’s state: the targets, the players, the room’s objects, the room window, the experience panel and the quick bar. Each is built for the frame and drawn into the inside of its window’s frame; a click on what it drew is found by asking the widget what a click there would do.
