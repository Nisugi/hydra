Module hub 

Source
Expand description

The hub, in a terminal: every character Hydra runs, at a glance, in four tabs – Live, the characters playing, each a card drawn from the card design (card.rs), the shared streams’ chat below them; Offline, those that ended this run, then the roster’s characters not playing, to start; Teams, roster characters started together; and New login, a login by account.

Tab and Shift+Tab, or 1 to 4, go between the tabs, as does a click on a tab’s name; the line under the tabs says the keys of the one shown. A password typed here goes to the binary in a [Password], which never prints, and leaves the hub once asked for; the hub is printed by its Debug, and never shows one.
