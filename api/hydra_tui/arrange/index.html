Module arrange 

Source
Expand description

Arranging a character’s screen: its windows moved, sized, added, taken away, their tabs and frames changed, by keys or by the mouse.

While arranging, the layout is fitted to the terminal, so a cell moved is a cell on the screen. The window with the focus is the one arranged:

  • the arrows move it, Ctrl with them by five; Shift with them sizes it from its right and bottom edges;
  • Tab and Shift+Tab give the focus to the next window and the one before;
  • a adds a window, t a tab to this one, chosen from the catalog; x takes away the tab shown, d or Delete the window;
  • e edits its settings (.editwindow); b changes its frame; f fits the whole layout to this terminal, r lays it out afresh;
  • Enter, Escape or F2 is done, and the layout is kept.

With the mouse, a window’s top edge drags it, and its bottom-right corner sizes it. Out of arranging, any edge with its border drawn sizes the window and its top edge moves it, unless the window is locked; with the layout’s shared edges on, a window whose edge met the one sized is sized with it.
