The Uberbar: one panel of what a hunter glances at, beside the injury doll. Labels for the day (experience gained today, the rate an hour, the last pulse, the room’s number, today’s silver and bounty points, the Voln favor) and bars under them (the vitals, the next level, the mind, the stance, the burden, the profession’s resource, a sorcerer’s shadow essence, how far to the experience cap, the next ascension point). Each row is on or off on the widget’s page.

The vitals, the mind, the stance, the burden, the next level, the resources, the favor and the room are the model’s. The day’s numbers are the ledger’s: Facts read from it by whoever draws the panel, no oftener than FACTS_EVERY, so a frame never waits on the database.
