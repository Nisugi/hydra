Module targets

Expand description

The Targets list, as the window’s widget and the page’s panel both show it: which creatures in the room are targets, and the look the player chose for them on the widget’s page, read from the character’s layout file so the page shows what the window shows.

A line names a creature (whole, without its article, its noun, or its noun once the line is full), its statuses (each as the player says it, in its colour, the most important first and no more than chosen) and its health percent, in the order chosen; a thin bar under it as tall as chosen, its colour from each percent up.
