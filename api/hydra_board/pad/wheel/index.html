Module wheel 

Source
Expand description

The command wheel: a ring of slices held open with a button, one aimed with a stick and fired.

The file. The default wheel is [[controller_wheel]], each entry a slice; a named one [[controller_wheels.<name>]], opened by a button bound to controller_wheel:<name>; [controller_wheels_meta.<name>] (default for the default wheel) gives a wheel the stick that aims it and the angle its first slice sits at. A slice has a label and a command, or slices of its own: a folder, opened by aiming at it. It may have a colour, a width in degrees (span), a floor the stick must be pushed past to aim it (inner, a percent), and how it fires (fire_type: release, edge, retract, or none for a slice that only holds its place).

The ring. Slices with a width take it, at least MIN_SPAN; the rest share what is left; and the whole is scaled to close at 360. The first slice is centred straight up, or at the wheel’s start, and the rest follow clockwise. Inside a folder a Back slice is added last, and the ring turned so Back sits where back_slice says (down, unless it says otherwise or none); a folder with a slice of its own marked back is used as it is. A folder’s own start turns its ring, and only its, that much further: the wheel’s turn is the top ring’s alone.

Aiming. The slice under the stick, past its floor, is the candidate. Held on it Timing::aim_ms a leaf is committed; held on a folder or Back Timing::nav_ms the wheel goes into it or up out of it, and the stick must come back to the middle before anything is aimed again. A leaf fires as its fire type says: release, the committed one when the wheel’s button is let go; edge, the moment the stick is pushed past Timing::edge_threshold, with no wait; retract, once committed, when the stick is drawn back Timing::retract_delta from the furthest it reached. The stick back in the middle before the release fires nothing.
