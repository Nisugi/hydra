Expand description

The controller’s feel.

Fields§

§walk_right: bool

The right stick walks, and the left aims and scrolls.

§other_scrolls: bool

The stick that does not walk scrolls the window in use, or steps through interact mode.

§back_slice: String

Where Back sits in a folder: down, up-left and the rest, or none.

§deadzone: u8

How far a stick is pushed, in percent, before a slice is aimed.

§aim_dwell_ms: u32

Held on a leaf this long, in milliseconds, it is committed.

§nav_dwell_ms: u32

Held on a folder or Back this long, in milliseconds, the wheel goes in or out.

§fire_debounce_ms: u32

A fire this soon after the last, in milliseconds, is not sent.

§release_grace_ms: u32

The walking stick is stilled this long, in milliseconds, after a wheel closes.

§fire_mode: FireMode

How a leaf fires, for a slice that does not say.

§edge_threshold: u8

How far out, in percent, an edge leaf fires.

§retract_delta: u8

How far back from its furthest, in percent, a retract leaf fires.

§trigger_open_pct: u8

How far an analog trigger is pulled, in percent, to open its wheel.

§trigger_close_pct: u8

How far an analog trigger falls under, in percent, to let its wheel go.

§wheel_min_open_ms: u32

A wheel stays open at least this long, in milliseconds.
