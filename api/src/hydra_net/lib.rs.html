1//! hydra-net: the bottom layer.
2//!
3//! The pipe a session's bytes come through, the eAccess login that opens it, and
4//! the record of what went through it. Nothing here knows what a `Frame` is.
5//!
6//! - [`eaccess`]: account login (`docs/eaccess-spec.md`), producing a [`LaunchPayload`].
7//! - [`live`]: the game connection ([`LiveSource`]), with keepalive and the WebSocket
8//!   shim fallback when the game port is unreachable.
9//! - [`bytes`]: the [`ByteSource`] trait every source implements.
10//! - [`replay`], [`record`]: replaying recorded bytes, and recording them.
11//! - [`sink`]: writes a session's raw wire to `<char>-<stamp>.bytes` plus a small `.log`.
12//! - [`answering`]: a stand-in game for tests in this and higher crates.
13//! - [`eastern`]: the US Eastern clock the game's own day runs on.
14//! - [`trainer`]: the play.net skill manager, read and answered without a browser.
15
16pub mod answering;
17pub mod bytes;
18pub mod eaccess;
19pub mod eastern;
