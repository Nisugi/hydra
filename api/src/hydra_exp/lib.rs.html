1//! Experience tracked, passively: nothing is ever sent to the game.
2//!
3//! The mind-state bar the game sends on every absorption pulse carries the
4//! character's totals (experience, ascension experience, the field pool,
5//! what is left to the next level) and which bonuses are on. The model keeps
6//! the latest; this crate keeps the history. Each bar whose totals moved
7//! becomes one pulse row in the ledger, with the gain, the bonuses and the
8//! mind's fullness as the bar said them. Values the game states only in a
9//! report's text (Voln favor, a profession's resource points) are taken down
10//! whenever they cross the stream and have changed.
11//!
12//! - [`tracker`]: what a look at the state yields, pure.
13//! - [`recorder`]: the task on a session that looks and writes.
14//! - [`levels`]: the experience each level takes.
15//! - [`report`]: lines from rows.
16//! - [`analysis`]: the reports across time: by the hour, the day, the
17//!   hour of the day, the mind's state, the hunt, the bonus, and everyone.
18//! - [`command`]: `.exp`, parsed and answered.
19

26pub mod tracker;
