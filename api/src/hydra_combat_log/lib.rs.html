1//! Combat recorded, passively: nothing is ever sent to the game.
2//!
3//! The combat reader in the model assembles each chunk's attacks (their
4//! rolls, flares, hits and crits) and the facts beside them (statuses,
5//! spell losses, stuns, deaths). This crate writes them to the ledger as
6//! hunts: a hunt opens at the first combat event and closes after
7//! [`IDLE_TIMEOUT`] seconds without one, stamped at the last event's time,
8//! so time in town never pads it. `ours` on an attack or flare is the
9//! reader's word; `kill_credit` says how a creature's killer was chosen.
10//!
11//! - [`recorder`]: the facts into rows, on a connection of its own.
12//! - [`task`]: the recorder on a session, and its idle clock.
13//! - [`report`]: the hunts and their numbers, as lines.
14//! - [`detail`]: the trees: attacks and creatures one by one.
15//! - [`listing`]: every attack name or creature over a scope, a page at a time.
16//! - [`analytics`]: flares, defences, HP, control, aim, efficiency, compared.
17//! - [`command`]: `.cstats`, parsed and answered.
18//! - [`catalog`]: the reports named, for a window that lists them.
19
20pub mod analytics;
21pub mod catalog;
22pub mod command;
23pub mod detail;
24pub mod listing;
25pub mod recorder;
26pub mod report;
27pub mod task;
