Crate hydra_map 

Source
Expand description

The map, for the minimap: hydra-mapper’s gs.map built in, each of its areas laid out by hydra-mapper’s engine and kept (Atlas), each character followed across them (Follower), and what the minimap draws (MinimapView, MapScene). Loaded once for everyone who asks (shared): the minimaps, and travel (hydra-travel).

The map, its decoder and its locator come from the cena-map crate, which the layout engine reads too.
