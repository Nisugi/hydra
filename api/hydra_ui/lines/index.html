Module lines 

Source
Expand description

How the story and a stream show their lines, with no toolkit in it: a time on each line, at its start or its end, with seconds or not, on a 12- or 24-hour clock (XX:XX, XX:XX:XX, XX:XX:XX AM/PM); word wrap; and the story’s prompts and what the player typed, each shown or not. What a widget of lines draws is decided here, line by line (Prompts); how it is drawn is the frontend’s.

Hydra’s own answers (hydra_session::Notice) are said in a token by what they are about, and a linked table as runs whose links are Hydra’s commands, the game’s, or a web page.
