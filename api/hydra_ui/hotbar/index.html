Hotbars: bars of slots, each slot holding one of the player’s macros (crate::macros), which is the button: what it shows and what a press sends.

The file is hotbars.toml, in the data folder for every character and beside a character’s layout for that one; a character’s bar replaces every character’s bar of the same name, whole.

[[bars]]
name = "combat"
rows = 2                 # the grid: rows and columns of slots
columns = 6
button_width = 80        # each button's size, in points
button_height = 28

[[bars.buttons]]
macro = "hide"           # the macro the slot holds
slot = 3                 # where on the grid, 0 the top left

A button of its own, as a file written for Vellum or by an earlier Hydra has them – a label, a command, a nickname, a hotkey, states with their looks – still reads, and still looks and sends as it says: the first state whose condition holds styles it, its unset parts from the button’s default_style, then from the button. Hydra makes each such button a macro once (crate::macros::adopt), leaving the button only its slot and its macro.

A bar is a grid of slots. A button sits in its slot; one with none, or one whose slot is taken or off the grid, takes the first free slot, in the file’s order. A grid never hides a button: one too small grows rows until every button has a slot. A button is found by its id, its macro or its nickname, never by its slot, so moving it breaks nothing that presses it.

A button’s icon (icon.rs) is a sheet’s cell or an image of its own, scaled to the button; icon_mode says whether its face is its label, its icon, or both. A state’s look may show another icon, and a dimmed button’s is drawn grey. What else a file says that Hydra does not read is kept as it has it.
