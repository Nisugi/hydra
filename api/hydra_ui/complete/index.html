Finishing what is typed: a ghost and Tab.

The ghost is the rest of the newest line sent that begins with what is typed, drawn after it in the weak colour: a suggestion, never sent unless taken. Typing on redraws it from the new text; a letter that matches shortens it, one that does not takes it away. Enter sends only what was typed.

Tab takes the ghost. Before that, with Hydra’s symbol and one word typed, Tab completes the word to a command’s: the first that begins so, and each Tab after the next, round again at the end. A word after a command that takes one of a known few (a window’s name, say) is completed so too, and with no line sent before to suggest, the ghost is the first such word. With neither to do, Tab is the next-target key as it was.
