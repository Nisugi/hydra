Module dialog_layout 

Source
Expand description

A dialog’s controls placed as the game lays them out: each control’s own top and left taken from where its align says (nw from the left, n from the middle, ne from the right), then its anchors followed against the controls they name: anchor_top hangs it below one, anchor_left puts it right of one, and with anchor_right too it stretches between two. A control that names a column with anchor_left and no anchor_top flows down that column, below the column’s controls that are placed by their own top.

The places are in the game’s pixels, on a canvas the width of the game’s side panels; the windows scale them to their width, the terminal turns them into rows and their order.

<label id='ublog' value='Today:' anchor_left='ubinjury' align='n' top='4' left='5'/>
<label id='ublogv' value='1,234' anchor_left='ublog' top='4' left='0'/>
<label id='ubhour' value='AVG/Hr:' anchor_left='ubinjury' anchor_top='ublog' top='2'/>

Each control placed, in the order given, with the extent they reach to; none when no control’s hints say where it goes, so it is laid out in rows instead. declared_height is the dialog’s own height when its tag gives one: e, w and c are measured from its middle, s from its foot.
