Crate hydra_ui 

Source
Expand description

What a frontend shows of one character, with no toolkit in it.

  • Story: the game’s lines as the story window shows them, routed by the game’s own stream declarations, with its prompts and what was sent, and each stream’s own lines for a widget of it.
  • History: what was typed, for up and down.
  • validate_line: one command line, checked before it is sent.
  • HubRequest: what a hub asks of whoever runs the sessions.
  • PlayView: the room, the vitals, roundtime and cast time, and the connection, projected from a snapshot.
  • object_menu: an object’s menu, labelled from the game’s dictionary.
  • theme: every colour by its meaning, and the palette Hydra draws in.
  • bounty: a bounty in brief, for a bounty window kept small.
