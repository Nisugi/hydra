Module creature_line 

Source
Expand description

A creature’s own lines, marked by what they say: its death, a spell it is preparing, a special about to land, or its rising or shaking off a stun. A line that links a creature is matched against that creature’s messages in the bestiary (hydra_model::state::creature_message), and one that is one of them takes the kind’s preset, which the theme colours as it does the game’s own presets: the player writes no highlight for them.

The creature is found by its link’s noun, every bestiary creature with that noun tried in turn, and only its handful of messages of the kinds here compared, as text with placeholders rather than as patterns: a line with no creature in it costs nothing, and one with costs microseconds.

A creature the bestiary lacks, or a wording it does not record – a boon’s adjective before the name, say – leaves its line unmarked.

Functions§

mark
Mark line with its preset, if it is a creature’s line: every run of it without a preset of its own takes it. A link and a creature’s name keep their own colours over it (crate::run_token).
preset
The preset line takes, if it is a death, a spell prep, a special or a recovery of a creature it links.
