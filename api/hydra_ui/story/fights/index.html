Module fights 

Source
Expand description

How the story shows fights: for each kind of attacker – the character, its companions, other players, creatures – the game’s lines, the fight told briefly ([hydra_session::brief]), or neither; whether a brief block shows its rolls and its flares; and whether fights and loot are kept in the story as well as their streams, over what the stream rules say.

Every character’s choices are FILE, in the data folder; a character’s own, the fights section of its settings file, are over them, a choice at a time (Fights::over). The combat stream always has the game’s lines.
