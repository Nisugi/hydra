One kind of widget: what it shows.

Variants§

§

Story

The game’s text: the main stream, and the streams whose window is closed, where the game declared they go.

§

Health

Health, as a bar.

§

Mana

Mana, as a bar.

Stamina

Stamina, as a bar.

§

Spirit

Spirit, as a bar.

§

Vitals

The vitals stacked: a spirit orb with health, mana and stamina rings round it.

§

RightHand

What the right hand holds.

§

LeftHand

What the left hand holds.

§

Roundtime

The roundtime left.

§

CastTime

The cast time left.

§

FieldExperience

Field experience against its most, as a bar.

§

BloodPoints

The betrayer’s Blood Points, out of 100, as a bar.

§

Stun

The stun left, by the rounds the game gave.

§

Aim

Where the character aims: the right eye, the left leg.

§

Room

The room as the game describes it, joined: its name and number, its description with what is here, who else is, and the ways out; its parts chosen on its own page.

§

RoomTitle

The room’s name.

§

RoomDescription

The room’s description.

§

Creatures

The creatures in the room, each with its status.

§

Npcs

The room’s creatures that were not hostile when first seen.

§

Objects

What else is in the room.

§

Players

The players in the room, painted by the character’s triggers.

§

Exits

The ways out.

§

Hydra

Hydra’s own messages, never in the game’s text.

§

Input

The command line, where it is placed; with none placed, it is the bar at the foot of the window.

§

Hunt

What the hunt is doing, and why it waits.

§

Stance

The stance, as a bar.

§

Encumbrance

Encumbrance, as a bar.

§

EncumbranceDetail

Encumbrance, in the game’s sentence.

§

Mind

The mind’s fill of experience, as a bar.

§

NextLevel

How near the next level, as a bar.

§

Level

The level.

§

TrainingPoints

Physical and mental training points.

§

ConvertedTrainingPoints

Physical training points converted to mental, as the skills table’s footer says.

§

ExperienceTotals

The experience numbers: total, field, ascension, long-term, deeds.

§

Prepared

The spell prepared.

§

Society

The society and rank.

§

Resources

The profession’s resource, and its kin.

§

Objectives

Quests and bounties the game lists.

§

Indicator(Indicator)

One status indicator: stunned, hidden, poisoned, …

§

Statuses

The custom statuses the triggers lit, each with its time left .

§

Effects(Category)

One of the game’s lists of effects.

§

Compass

The room’s ways out, as a compass rose.

§

Injuries

The injury doll: each hurt part, wound over scar.

§

Uberbar

The Uberbar: the doll, the day’s numbers beside it and the bars under them, each row chosen on its page.

§

Combat

Who is fighting in the room: friends and foes.

§

Stream(String)

One of the game’s streams, by its id: thoughts, speech, logons, … or any other the character has received.

§

Spellbook

The spells the model has learned. Not offered in the Add-a-widget list (the game’s own Spells window does its work); kept so layouts holding one still load.

§

Reserve

What the character keeps in reserve.

§

Containers

The containers the game has shown, and what each holds.

§

Inventory

The whole inventory, as the game’s inventory manager gives it: every container, open or not, with weights and capacities.

§

Trainer

The skill trainer: the character’s skills and goals, from the play.net skill manager goals opens, changed and applied here.

§

Bestiary

The creature codex, browsed: what .bestiary answers.

§

Pulse

When the next pulse comes.

§

WorldEvents

The world events under way.

§

EbonGateMerchants

The Ebon Gate merchants at work.

§

Dialog(String)

One of the game’s own dialogs, by its id: the Betrayer panel, the combat panel, one an event adds; offered once the game has sent it.

§

Minimap

The area the character is in, laid out, following it.

§

Travel

The places .go2 save keeps, to go to by a click.

§

Notes

The player’s notes, read and edited in place.

§

Quick

The quick bar: the game’s three bars and the player’s own pages, one at a time.

§

Hotbar

One of the player’s hotbars, its buttons restyled by the character’s state.

§

Missing

Every tracked effect not up, in every list.

§

Timers

The Timers panel: the effect lists merged, or only the tracked effects among them.

§

Chip

One effect chip: a mark and a few words over the effects it gathers, with a popup listing them.

§

GameState

Everything the model holds for the character, as it prints itself: for troubleshooting.

§

Lag

Lag: how long the game takes to answer what is typed, the network’s time and its own.

§

Card(CardPart)

A part of a hub card that is the hub’s own (crate::card): offered by the cards’ editor alone, never on a play window’s list.

§

Field(Vec<String>)

One value of the game state, by its path through the state’s print : offered by the cards’ editor.

§

CustomIndicator

An indicator the player makes, lit by a rule of their own : its name, rule and look kept in the layout by its id, so any number may be placed.

impl Widget

Source

pub fn all() -> Vec<Widget>

Every kind the list always offers, in its order. Streams and dialogs the game sends besides are added by the list as they arrive.

Source

pub fn is_effects(&self) -> bool

One of the effect widgets, which show the tracked effects.

Source

pub fn is_effects_window(&self) -> bool

An effects window: one of the lists, Missing or the Timers panel, each following what every effects window does where its own page leaves it unset.

Source

pub fn is_room_part(&self) -> bool

One of the room’s parts, listed with the Room.

Source

pub fn is_hidden(&self) -> bool

Not offered by the list at all: kept so a layout that holds one still loads (Spellbook, whose work the game’s Spells window does; the hunt pane, which waits on Lich), or a game dialog Hydra already shows better as its own widget.

Source

pub fn type_key(&self) -> String

The key a theme gives a kind of widget its own face by: its name, lowercase, words joined by _ – story, room, thoughts, round_time.

Source

pub fn name(&self) -> Cow<'static, str>

What a player calls it: a standalone window’s title, and its name in a list.

Source

pub fn group(&self) -> Group

Its category: in the Add-a-widget list, and in Settings’ list of widgets.

Source

pub fn stacks(&self) -> bool

Whether it may be a tab of a stack: the story, the streams and Hydra’s own messages, which are read; nothing else stacks.

Source

pub fn kin(&self) -> Kin

Which kinds are alike for a stack’s sake. Only the read ones stack (Self::stacks). Coarser than the categories, and unchanged by them.

Source

pub fn emblem(&self, second: bool) -> Option<&'static str>

The name of its emblem in an emblem style, when it is a stat’s bar: health, field_experience; second, stamina’s lungs and the pulse’s bell in place of the winged boot and the watch.

Source

pub fn emblems(&self) -> Option<(&'static str, &'static str)>

What its two emblems are called, when its stat has a second.

Source

pub fn bar_token(&self) -> Option<Token>

The palette’s token its bar fills with, when it is one bar.

Source

pub fn has_lines(&self) -> bool

Whether it draws lines the Find bar looks through: the story, or a stream.

Source

pub fn is_story(&self) -> bool

Whether it is one character’s story, which never follows another character: no window mixes two characters’ story. Hydra’s messages count as its story, and so does each of its streams.

Source

pub fn size(&self) -> [f32; 2]

The size it would like, across and down in points, when nothing else says: a bar or a hand is one line, a list a few, the story as much as it is given (its geometry is the widget’s to say).
