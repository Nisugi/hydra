Module frame 

Source
Expand description

A window frame: a kit of four corners and four looping runs, drawn round a window, a card or the play window, flush with its edge.

A kit is a folder under the frames folder’s kits/<pack>/<style>/, in the data folder’s global/images, as a pack installs it: the corners tl.png, tr.png, bl.png and br.png, square; the runs top.png and bottom.png, looping across, and left.png and right.png, looping down; and kit.toml, which gives the frame’s thickness (bar), how far its decoration reaches inward over the window (overhang), the corners’ side (corner) and a run’s length before it repeats (run), each in the pictures’ pixels. A kit is named by its pack and style, pack/style.

lay places the pieces round a window at a chosen thickness, every measure scaled by that thickness over bar. Each run is bar + overhang deep against its own side, its outer edge on the window’s edge, repeated along the side from its start, the last repeat cut off. The corners go over the ends, each with its outer corner on the window’s; a side shorter than two corners cuts each at the side’s middle. Nothing reaches past the window’s edge: the window’s content keeps the thickness in from it, and the overhang lies over the content, in the window’s top half only: below its middle nothing is drawn deeper than the thickness. The drawing is a toolkit’s: this module only says where each piece goes and which part of its picture it shows.

The pieces of a kit measured as measures says, round window at thickness points, in the order they are drawn: the runs, then the corners over them. None for a window or a thickness of nothing.
