The sorter: a container look shown as its contents by item type.

A look such as

In the mahogany box you see a bright gold ingot, some silver coins, a smooth amber wand, a piece of brown jade, a steel lockpick, a pinch of electrum dust and a tar black tourmaline.

is shown as

In the mahogany box:
valuable (1): a bright gold ingot
other (1): some silver coins
wand (1): a smooth amber wand
gem (3): a pinch of electrum dust, a piece of brown jade, a tar black tourmaline
lockpick (1): a steel lockpick

or, given a width, each type’s label on a line of its own and its items under it in columns that fit the width.

The look carries every item as an <a exist= noun=> link, so the line is what is sorted, and each item keeps its link. The look leaves out what the game says after an item’s name (a pair of heavy pewter earrings with a cluster of jasper accents); the <inv> lines the game sends before it have it, and Listed keeps it for the look that follows.

  • What is a look: a line starting In the or On the with you see, or Peering into with you see , and never one naming both an In the and an On the (is_container_look). The first object link is the container; the rest are the items.
  • Only a whole list is sorted: after the container you see and an article; between items , or and and an article; after the last a . that ends the line. Any other shape is shown as it came: the herb kit’s look goes on past its list (The kit contains DOSEs of ...), and sorting it would lose the doses. Text after an item’s link that is not a sentence stays with the item: leather journal embossed with a peacock feather.
  • The type is every type the item’s noun and name have ([classify]), joined by commas, or other. Types join in name order.
  • Names: each item whole, as the <inv> lines say it: its article, its name, and what follows the name; or, with long names off, its name alone and what the look says after it. An item containing something says it in brackets, a glass vial (some red liquid); a crystalline flask is named by what it holds.
  • Order: types as first seen; within one, items by the last word of the whole name; the same item twice is one, with its count.

Listed
What the game’s <inv> lines say around each item’s name, by its exist id: a before pair of heavy pewter earrings, with a cluster of jasper accents after. A look’s container is emptied (<clearContainer>) and listed again just before the look, so each emptying starts afresh.

Constants§

MAX_SORTED_RUNS
Runs a line may have and still be sorted; past it the line is shown as it came. The longest look in a month of play named 108 items, 217 runs.

Functions§

is_container_look
Whether text, a story line’s plain text, is a container look: a cheap first test, sort also requires a whole list.
sort
The lines a container look is shown as when sorted: a header, then each type’s items, as a list or, given width in characters, in columns. Given listing, each item is named long, as it says, or as the look does where it says nothing; without, short, its name and the look’s own text after it. None when line is not a look, or not a whole list.
