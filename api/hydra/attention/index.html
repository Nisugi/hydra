Module attention

Expand description

A highlight’s sound, speech and notification, played on a thread of its own: the audio device is opened, and lives, on one thread; so does the computer’s voice.

Speech is the computer’s own voice (tts), opened at the first words as the audio device is at the first sound, and said at the window’s volume. A build without the speech feature says nothing.

One occurrence, several characters. Three characters in one room hear one shout. A call another character made within a second – the same highlight, sound and words – is that occurrence again, and passes silently. The same character calling twice is two occurrences, which its cooldown has already let through.

The sound is found by its file name in the sounds folder, <data>/sounds, as written or with .wav, .mp3, .ogg or .flac after it. A path is never followed (hydra_ui::highlight::sound). The audio device is opened at the first sound, not before: opening it can take ten seconds on a machine with none.
