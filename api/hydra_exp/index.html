Crate hydra_exp 

Source
Expand description

Experience tracked, passively: nothing is ever sent to the game.

The mind-state bar the game sends on every absorption pulse carries the character’s totals (experience, ascension experience, the field pool, what is left to the next level) and which bonuses are on. The model keeps the latest; this crate keeps the history. Each bar whose totals moved becomes one pulse row in the ledger, with the gain, the bonuses and the mind’s fullness as the bar said them. Values the game states only in a report’s text (Voln favor, a profession’s resource points) are taken down whenever they cross the stream and have changed.

  • tracker: what a look at the state yields, pure.
  • recorder: the task on a session that looks and writes.
  • levels: the experience each level takes.
  • report: lines from rows.
  • analysis: the reports across time: by the hour, the day, the hour of the day, the mind’s state, the hunt, the bonus, and everyone.
  • command: .exp, parsed and answered.

Modules§

analysis
The reports that look across time rather than add a span up: by the hour, by the day, by the hour of the day, by the mind’s state, by the hunt, by the bonus, and every character at once. Each is said as blocks (hydra_report): a chart, a table, a card of facts, a note.
catalog
The reports named, for a window that lists them: each with the words that ask for it, a line saying what it shows, and what it takes. Only the reports: what writes (addgain, clean, reset) is typed.

.exp on the command line: what was asked, and its answer from the ledger.
levels
The experience each level takes, and the level a total has reached.
recorder
The recorder: a task on a session that hears the bars, looks at the state when the game has said something about experience, and writes what changed.
report
The reports: lines from rows, with no file and no game.
tracker
The bars heard and the looks taken, and the rows they yield.
