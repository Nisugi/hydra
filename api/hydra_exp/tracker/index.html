Source
Expand description

The bars heard and the looks taken, and the rows they yield.

The tracker keeps an Experience of its own, fed every mind-state bar as it comes (Tracker::heard), so a burst of bars is still one pulse each, in order. A look (Tracker::look) compares those totals with the totals last recorded and yields a pulse when they moved; it also reads the character’s state for the guild’s count and for favor and resource points, yielding a snapshot when one changed. The first bar sets the baseline and yields no pulse, since the gain before it is unknown.

§How a pulse’s source is told

The game’s lines say it. The guild taking a task as done counts up in the model (bounty.finished): a gain within BOUNTY_WINDOW of that count rising is the task’s reward. A brooch rubbed counts up too (brooch_rubs), and [You have instantly absorbed N experience points!] sums what was absorbed at once (instant_absorbed), whatever brought it. A pulse records that sum since the last pulse as its instant, and its source is bounty, brooch, instant or normal in that order of what the lines said. No threshold guesses.
