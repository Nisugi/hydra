Hydra

Examples

Each example is a trigger made from New, shown with a line from the game pasted into Try it, so you can see what it does before it ever meets the game. Change the names and words to your own.

Colour your friends’ names

New → Colour words. Look for the names, with | between them, and tick Whole words only, so Tevye does not light up inside another word. Choose the colour under Show it.

Hide lines you never want to see

New → Hide lines. Look for words every such line holds. Lich’s LNet chat starts each of its lines with [LNet].

To keep the chat, but out of the story, move it instead: see Give a chat its own window.

Know when someone speaks to you

New → Alert me. The game writes speech meant for you as Speaking to <you>, <them> says, so look for Speaking to and your name. Under Tell me: a sound, a banner of the line, and a notification, so it reaches you with Hydra behind other windows. A cooldown of 10 seconds keeps a long talk from ringing on every line.

Count down a stun

New → Start a timer. The game says how many rounds: You are stunned for 2 rounds!. A pattern keeps the number, (\d+), and the timer runs $1 times 5 seconds. Under Tell me, speech says Stunned, for when you are looking elsewhere.

It counts down in the Debuffs list, and in the Timers widget, which gathers every list.

Time a spell

New → Start a timer. Look for the spell’s message as it comes up, A light blue glow surrounds you., and give the timer as long as your casting lasts. Down message ends it when the spell wears off early: The light blue glow leaves you.

Shorten a long line

New → Anything. A pattern takes the line apart into groups, and Write instead puts it back together, shorter:

The gameThe story
Speaking to Tevye, Nexushealbot says, "I'm running low on mana."Nexushealbot to Tevye: "I'm running low on mana."

Give a chat its own window

New → Anything. Look for [LNet], and under Show it, Move to a stream named lnet. Once a line has gone there, the play window’s Layout menu, Add a widget, offers an Lnet window, under Other game windows. Place it where you like.

Answer the game

New → Send a command. When your spell is ready, cast it:

A trigger sends only once the character is in the game and ready, never while it logs in. Its Cooldown, 3 seconds unless you change it, is the pace of its sends: at 0 it sends for every line it finds.

Watch your health

New → Watch my character. There are no words to look for: under When, the condition vital health < 50 % is the whole trigger. It plays a sound and shows Health is low each time your health falls under half. Try it has no line to try: the sentence at the top says what it does.

Light a status while a spell lasts

Two triggers: one lights a status, one puts it out. New → Mark a flag or status, look for the spell coming up, and name the Status: Warded.

Then a second, looking for The light blue glow leaves you., with the same status and clears it ticked. The Custom statuses widget shows Warded while the spell lasts, and any trigger can ask for it under When: indicator Warded. A flag works the same way, but shows nowhere: it is for other triggers alone.
