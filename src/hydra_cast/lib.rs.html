1//! Typed spell casting: with a character's caster on, a spell number or one
2//! of its spell aliases typed, with a target and a count if wanted, becomes a
3//! cast.
4//!
5//! ```text
6//! 901                  incant 901
7//! 901 kobold           prepare 901, then cast kobold
8//! 901 second kobold 3  prepare 901, then cast second kobold 3
9//! zap kobold           the same, with `zap` an alias of 901
10//! ```
11//!
12//! What a cast does is worked out here, from the line, the character's
13//! settings ([`settings`]), the spell table and what the character is and
14//! has ([`mod@plan`]); the game's answers are read by [`answers`].
15//!
16//! - [`mod@request`]: a typed line as a cast asked for, or not.
17//! - [`mod@plan`]: the checks a cast passes or is refused by, its stance, its
18//!   verb, and the commands it sends.
19//! - [`answers`]: what the game says to a prepare, a cast, a release and a
20//!   stance, as each needs to know.
21

23pub mod driver;
24pub mod plan;
25pub mod request;
26pub mod settings;
