Module interact 

Source
Expand description

Interact mode: the room’s creatures, objects, players and ways out, stepped through without the mouse.

One thing has the focus, in one of the four kinds. A step moves it within its kind, wrapping; a kind step moves it to the next kind that has anything, from its first. The focus is kept by the thing’s id (a way out’s direction), so the room’s list changing does not move it off what it was on. Acting on it asks the game for its menu, as a click on its link does, or walks the way out, which leaves interact mode.

A macro’s <target_id> and <target_noun> are the thing in focus.
