Module touch 

Source
Expand description

The web page’s touch wheel: a character’s wheel as its controller files hold it – every character’s, its own over it – for a phone to hold open with its thumb.

The page is given the wheel’s shape alone: labels, colours, widths, folders, where Back sits, and the feel it aims with. A slice fired is named by its place, and its command is read here, from the files, so nothing the page says is sent as a command of its own. A slice that needs interact mode’s focus, which a phone has not, is refused.
