Lines the player types for Hydra, not for the game: a command symbol marks a line as a command, and such a line is never sent to the game.

§The symbol decides, not the word

A line that begins with the symbol is Hydra’s, whether or not anything knows the word. .go22 bank is a mistyped .go2, and sending it to the game says .go22 bank in the room. So an unrecognised command is answered with “I do not know that”, and the game never hears it.

The symbol is . by default: ; is Lich’s, and stays Lich’s. It is the character’s to change, on the settings menu’s General page (Settings), and a running character takes a new one at once.

Lich’s own symbol sits beside it, a setting of its own that defaults to ;: a line it marks is the player’s Lich’s, and neither Hydra’s nor the game’s (crate::lich). It must be the one the player’s Lich itself answers to.

§This crate knows nothing about commands

It holds the symbol, splits the line, and asks whoever registered, with who sent the line. What .history means is the binary’s.
